**Sport Track (Body → Tracker, `apps/sport/index.html`).** Restyled in place (same data, ids and sync): it draws itself from `--st-*` colours, which `core/embedTheme.ts` points at Life's tokens (dark, light, accent). Today: a hero (a ring of today's reps against the targets, "Next up", one **Start / Continue workout** button) with one "Today's plan" line (what's left, the sleep note, the step-up, and Full · Lighter 70% · Short 50%); exercise cards with big numbers, a bar, one quick-add (about a fifth of the target, or what's left), Done and a ⋯ menu (+5/+10/+25, add a number, take back 10, rest timer, edit); a Water card (bottle fill, glass / bottle / undo; 1 L, goal and reset in its ⋯); a This week card (sessions vs goal, the 7 days, streak, sessions, points paid). The rest timer is a box opened from a button or a menu; "I did all of these…" is a quiet footer action that still asks for the real numbers. Rarely used things (exercises & targets, settings, rest day, reset today) are in the top ⋯. History: calendar + log a session + recent sessions; Progress: 30-day charts, records, Telegram tips. **Library, levels, badges (SP2):** the tested logic is TypeScript in `src/core/sportMoves.ts` (56 real bodyweight moves in 6 groups: how-to steps, the common mistake, muscles, difficulty 1–5, and an animated demo as a few key poses), `sportFigure.ts` (draws a pose: two-bone IK keeps limbs their length, moving limbs swing round the joint, `boxOf` frames a demo), `sportLadders.ts` (families are ladders; `suggestHarder` after 3 sessions in a row at target, "not yet" for a week, `startTarget` for the harder move) and `sportBadges.ts` (20 badges from the day log, sessions and records; **no points**). `src/core/sportKit.ts` is bundled into `apps/sport/sportkit.js` (`window.SportKit`) with `npm run build:sport`; `sportKit.test.ts` fails when the bundle is stale. The page draws the figure as SVG (a ~40 fps timer loop, only for demos on screen, paused under a box, stopped when hidden). Today: exercise cards show the demo (plays on hover, tap = How-to sheet) and Level n/N; the hero shows the next move moving, and when all is done a celebration (ring burst, confetti, the day's numbers, "Try 1 set" of a harder move or a cool-down); a "Try something harder" card (Add 1 set / Swap / Not yet: new exercises through the same add path, the old one is turned off, never deleted) and a "Next badge" card. Tabs Library (your ladders + all moves) and Badges (earned / still to get, an unlock pop with confetti; prefers-reduced-motion skips confetti and hover play). Workout mode: the big demo, a rolling counter, a rest ring over the demo, a timer for holds (unit `sec`), a finish screen. The stat that showed "Points –" shows real points paid today, else reps this week. New fields in the sport state: `exercises[].move`, `badges` (id → day shown), `notYet` (move → day); `mergeSport` keeps both devices' badges and the later "not yet". `--st-fig` is the figure colour (darker in light mode). Smoke step "Sport Track Today" (cards, demo, How-to sheet, Library, Badges). **Sets, strength and the week plan (SP3).** Demos: 27 moves use two real photo frames (start, end) from the Free Exercise DB (github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db, public domain under the Unlicense; license text and credits in `apps/sport/photos/NOTICE.md`), cropped to 480×384 WebP and bundled in `apps/sport/photos/` (about 670 KB, lazy-loaded, no hotlinking); the mapping and crops are `core/sportPhotos.ts`, `scripts/sport-photos.mjs` remakes them. They cross-fade start → end on a loop (Slow/Normal in the How-to sheet; reduced motion shows both frames side by side). The other moves are drawn as a mannequin (`core/sportDraw.ts`: tapered limbs with joints, a shaped torso, outlined, worked muscles in the accent). Sets: `sets[day][exerciseId] = [{ reps, kg?, at, id }]` in the sport state (`core/sportSets.ts`: parse "3x15 push-ups" / "push-ups 15 14 12", best set, hard sets per muscle group, weekly volume, `importSets` from old sessions and records; day totals stay volume only). A card's quick add logs one set; "Sets…" types several; "Add a total" stays volume. Progress (`core/sportProgress.ts`, setting `sportySettings.progressMode`: `grow` default, or `endurance` = the old +5% step-up): double progression in a rep range (8–15 push/pull, 10–20 legs/core, seconds for holds; lower for hard moves), 3 working sets, aim +1 rep a session; the top in all sets 2 sessions running unlocks the next ladder step (a "Level up!" moment once, `levelSeen`; Move up swaps like before), under the bottom twice suggests the easier step, 10+ days off starts at about 70% (or one step down) and ramps +3. In Build muscle `ex.target` follows sets × aim (the old one is kept in `endTarget` and comes back with Endurance). Strength (`core/sportStrength.ts`): a set's score is 100 × 1.2^(ladder step) × (1 + reps/30), so a harder move counts as growth; the Strength tab (was Progress) shows "X% stronger than 4 weeks ago", a score per muscle group, best set over time per ladder (one line per step), hard sets this week on a body map (10–20 zone), then the old volume charts and records. Plan (`core/sportProgram.ts`, the Plan tab, settings `planMinutes` 20/30/45, `planPerWeek` 3/4, `planCool`, `planFollow`): 3 full-body A/B or 4 upper/lower sessions, 4–6 exercises × 3 sets, rest 60–120 s (pairs when time is short), 3-min warm-up, optional cool-down, estimated minutes that fit; your own ladder steps are used, missing slots get a library move added as `plan: true, active: false`. Today then shows today's session (rest days say so, with Train anyway; the Rest day button works as before); workout mode follows it: warm-up ring, "Set N of 3 · aim", Set done, a rest ring, next. Celebrations: a full-screen canvas burst, a ring that draws and explodes, rolling stats (vs last time), "New record!" / "Level up!" banners and the Life sound kit (habit, success, levelUp, reward); about 2 s, a tap or Esc skips, none of the motion with reduced motion. Import from Telegram (History card and the top ⋯): pick the Telegram Desktop HTML export(s) of the old sport bot chat; `core/sportTelegramImport.ts` reads it in the browser (bot day cards like "Push-ups (123/117 reps)", "press 0 / 35", your logs like "47pushups"; the highest per exercise per day), a preview lets you untick days, then past days merge into the day log (higher number wins) with one history entry each (`source: 'telegram import'`, `paid: 0`, no sets), badges they earn are noted quietly. `mergeSport` joins `sets` by id, keeps the higher `best`, and `levelSeen`. The sport bot logs sets too ("3x15 push-ups", "push-ups 15 14 12": `parseTelegramText` → `exerciseSets`, `core/sportBot.ts` adds them to Sport Track and replies with the best set and the next level). Points are unchanged: sets, levels, records and imports pay nothing.

## Too easy means harder (2026-11-07)

Pure logic in `core/sportBoost.ts` (tests: `sportBoost.test.ts`, pinned dates), shown through `sportkit.js`. Points unchanged (nothing here pays).
- **Today strip** (`#lv-strip`): the rank ("Trainee → Fighter, 8 pts", big emblem) and one big level badge per exercise ("Push-ups · Level 8 of 15 · Machine", bar to the next level and its requirement); a tap opens Progress → Levels on that ladder. Cards show the level large (`.lv-big`).
- **Automatic harder variation** (`autoVariation`): when a target is above the move's range (e.g. 3×21 at a top of 20), Strength hits 3×8, or a best set says the move is far too easy, Today switches to the hardest variation you could still do at the bottom of its range (about 70% of the reps per step, up to 2 steps, 4 with a best set), banner "Level up: Diamond push-ups 3×8 (your 3×21 push-ups are now your warm-up)" with Undo (the old move is only turned off; Undo sets "Not yet" for 7 days). Once a day per exercise, never mid-workout, not in an easy week.
- **Too easy / Too hard** on every card (`shiftLevel`): a level up (two for a second "Too easy" in 7 days, `state.tooEasy`), the new target set (`state.tune`, today's goal), Undo. A move at the top of its ladder says to slow the tempo (3 s down, pause).
- **Effort** (`state.effort[day][exId]`: Easy / Good / Hard after a finished exercise): easy at the top of the range is enough for the harder move (1 session, not 2) and adds +2 reps next time; hard repeats the target.
- **Best set** (`state.bestSets[family] = { move, reps, day }`, form in the strip and in "Why this level"): counts as 3 working sets of 60% of it on that day and the day before, and a declared easier move counts toward harder levels at 70% of the reps per step (`transferReps`), so 50 regular push-ups is about Level 13, not 8.
- **Strength mode** (`progressMode: 'strength'`, the default since routine v4; Build muscle and Simple totals stay): range 5–8, 4 sets, 150 s rests, a harder move as soon as 3×8 is reached. The harder home moves (one-arm-assisted push-ups, feet-elevated rows, dragon flag) are not in the library yet; at the top of a ladder the card advises slow tempo instead.
- **Thin routine**: 3 moves or fewer shows "A balanced 5–6 move set" (`balancedSet`: push, doorway rows, split squats, plank, hollow hold or a harder finisher) with "Add these N" / "Not now" (7 days).

## Growth coach (2026-11-07)

**Sport now lives in Ben.** The app's bots (webBots, telegramBot) take sport logging: "3x15 push-ups", "push-ups 15 14 12", "8 push-ups +10 kg", "plank 3x45s" and "best set push-ups 50" (`parseSportSay` in `core/sportBot.ts`, read by `botCommands.ts`). Ben replies with a receipt: what was logged, the best set, the level, the next target and the coach's note for next time. A best set is remembered (`state.bestSets`), not logged as a workout. The old separate Python bot is another repo and is left alone.

**The rules, in code** (`core/sportCoachRules.ts`, tests `sportCoachRules.test.ts` with made-up weeks and pinned dates). Every suggestion comes from them and nothing, not even the weekly AI review, may break them:
1. 10-20 hard sets a week per muscle group, on at least 2 days (`muscleLoad`, last 7 days).
2. Sets stop 1-2 reps short of failure (said on every first session and in the card).
3. Progressive overload in a fixed order (`overload`): reps up to the top of the range, then the next harder variation, and at the top of a ladder added weight (5 kg first, then +2 kg), then back to the bottom of the range (5 in Strength mode). Two tough sessions: step back with a slow 3-1-1 tempo.
4. Rest suits the reps (`restFor`: 5-8 reps 2.5-3 min, 9-12 about 1.75, 13-20 about 1.25, holds 1 min).
5. Deload when 6 hard weeks in a row, or 4 with a stall (`deloadCheck`).
6. Protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg and sleep 7+ h come from the Body data (`sportBodyNow` in `main/bodyPlus.ts`, sent to the page with `LIFE_SPORT_INSIGHT`).

**Your coach card** (top of Today, `#your-coach`): the single most important thing this week ("Add 2 sets of pulling: your back is behind"), today's exact session (variation, sets × reps, weight or tempo, rest), one line why, protein/sleep lines, this week's plan. **After every log** the page and the bots call `adjustAfterSession` (`sportAdjust.ts`): progression, step back, swap, +1 set where a muscle is under 10. **Weekly review** (`sportReview.ts`): the page writes the rules' program each Monday (`state.weekProgram`, source `code`); the first training question or "sport review" to Ben lets the strong model (existing `ask` path, kind `weekly`) rewrite it. The output is forced inside the rules (targets 10-20, 2+ sessions, a due deload always on), every number must be in the facts, and `benGround` removes invented times and events; with no model or a bad answer the code's plan stays. **Ask Ben** (`sportAsk.ts`, `isTrainingQuestion`): "why am I not growing?", "is 4×8 archer enough?", rest, deload, protein, answered from his data and the rules.

**Best-set onboarding** (`sportOnboard.ts`): on first open, and from "Re-check my levels" (top menu and the coach card), max reps in one set for push-ups, squats, sit-ups, rows and plank seconds. `placeBest` puts each exercise on the variation where about 10-14 reps is the max, so 4×5-8 is hard (50 push-ups: archer push-ups 4×7). Old exercises are only turned off. **Imported days** (`state.importedDays`, set by `mergeTelegramDays`) are history, streak and volume only: they never raise a level (`isImported` in `sportLevels.ts` / `sportCoach.ts`). **Weekly strength test** (card button when 7 days passed): one max set per main move, saved as real sets flagged `test`; updates levels and the chart, pays no points. **Strength charts**: best set and level over 6 months per ladder, with "+X% in N weeks" from best sets and tests, never totals. **Weighted sets**: type "8 +10 kg" in a card's set box (or "8 push-ups +10 kg" to Ben); sets keep `kg`.

**New moves**: one-arm-assisted push-ups (top of the push-up ladder), pseudo-planche leans (plank ladder, holds), feet-elevated rows and one-arm rows, hollow body rocks, dragon flag negatives, box pistols between Bulgarian and shrimp squats (archer, shrimp, pistol already existed). One photo (box pistol, Free Exercise DB); the rest are drawn. Smoke step "Sport Track onboarding".

## Simple mode is the default (2026-11-07)

The owner found the coach too complicated ("I want back my push-ups and abs and stuff and just be able to increase the difficulty"). **Simple mode is now the default** for everyone; the coach is opt-in. Pure logic in `core/sportSimple.ts` (tests: `sportSimple.test.ts`), page switch `coachOn()` (`sportySettings.coachMode === true`).
- **Simple Today**: your own active exercises and daily targets. Each card shows the name, demo photo, today's count and target, a big Log (types a number), +5 / +10, Done, and a small "Harder ▸". Harder opens three choices: **+5 reps** (target +5), **+1 set** (target + a third of it), **Harder version** (the next ladder step, only that one exercise is swapped; the old one is turned off, never deleted). "Easier ▸" undoes the last step. The choice is remembered per exercise in `ex.harder`. A tiny "Level n of N" sits in the card's ⋯ menu. - **Double progression** (`levelStatus`): sets × rep range at an effort target ("stop 1–2 reps before failure"). The level is complete when every working set reaches the top of the range in **2 sessions in a row**, with no set tapped Hard and no set marked "form broke" (untapped counts as OK, as before). Reason text: "3×12 easy twice", "3×12 twice with 1–2 reps left" or "3×12 in 2 sessions in a row". Easy both times and 25%+ past the top offers two levels up.
- **Offered, never automatic** (`offerOf`, `PlanItem.offer`): the next variation is offered; **Apply** (`applyOffer` → `setLevel`, the new level counts from today) or **Keep** (`keepOffer` → `declinedOn`; it comes back after a newer session at the top). The roadmap's Move up / Not yet is the same choice.
- **Make easier / Make harder** (`shiftLevel`, `shiftMove`, `shiftWorkout`): one level at once, per move or the whole session. Logs are never touched; a level you were on before keeps the day you first reached it (`setLevel(..., keep)`), so its sessions still count; going down waits for a newer session before offering the harder move again.
- **Placement for existing users** (`bestOf(st, move, today)`): logged sets, best sets you told Sport Track, and your daily totals of the last 60 days (as the first set of an even split; 130 push-ups a day ≈ 22 a set → Diamond push-ups); Telegram-imported days never place you. A best set of 50 push-ups → Archer push-ups. Old logs keep counting in stats and badges.
- **Dose = sets** (`doseSets`): Full; Lighter one set less a move; Short 2 sets a move. The range and effort stay. The dose is today's readiness dose (`bodyPlus.ready` / `setDose`, the same setting Sport Track's Classic tracker reads).
- **Form check**: a set may carry `form: 'off'` (`SetEntry`, kept by `mergeSets`); such a session does not count toward the unlock.
