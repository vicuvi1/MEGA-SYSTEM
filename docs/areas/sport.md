The page draws the figure as SVG (a ~40 fps timer loop, only for demos on screen, paused under a box, stopped when hidden). Today: exercise cards show the demo (plays on hover, tap = How-to sheet) and Level n/N; the hero shows the next move moving, and when all is done a celebration (ring burst, confetti, the day's numbers, "Try 1 set" of a harder move or a cool-down); a "Try something harder" card (Add 1 set / Swap / Not yet: new exercises through the same add path, the old one is turned off, never deleted) and a "Next badge" card. Tabs Library (your ladders + all moves) and Badges (earned / still to get, an unlock pop with confetti; prefers-reduced-motion skips confetti and hover play). Workout mode: the big demo, a rolling counter, a rest ring over the demo, a timer for holds (unit `sec`), a finish screen. The stat that showed "Points –" shows real points paid today, else reps this week. New fields in the sport state: `exercises[].move`, `badges` (id → day shown), `notYet` (move → day); `mergeSport` keeps both devices' badges and the later "not yet". `--st-fig` is the figure colour (darker in light mode). Smoke step "Sport Track Today" (cards, demo, How-to sheet, Library, Badges). **Sets, strength and the week plan (SP3).** Demos: 27 moves use two real photo frames (start, end) from the Free Exercise DB (github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db, public domain under the Unlicense; license text and credits in `apps/sport/photos/NOTICE.md`), cropped to 480×384 WebP and bundled in `apps/sport/photos/` (about 670 KB, lazy-loaded, no hotlinking); the mapping and crops are `core/sportPhotos.ts`, `scripts/sport-photos.mjs` remakes them. They cross-fade start → end on a loop (Slow/Normal in the How-to sheet; reduced motion shows both frames side by side). The other moves are drawn as a mannequin (`core/sportDraw.ts`: tapered limbs with joints, a shaped torso, outlined, worked muscles in the accent). Sets: `sets[day][exerciseId] = [{ reps, kg?, at, id }]` in the sport state (`core/sportSets.ts`: parse "3x15 push-ups" / "push-ups 15 14 12", best set, hard sets per muscle group, weekly volume, `importSets` from old sessions and records; day totals stay volume only). A card's quick add logs one set; "Sets…" types several; "Add a total" stays volume. Progress (`core/sportProgress.ts`, setting `sportySettings.progressMode`: `grow` default, or `endurance` = the old +5% step-up): double progression in a rep range (8–15 push/pull, 10–20 legs/core, seconds for holds; lower for hard moves), 3 working sets, aim +1 rep a session; the top in all sets 2 sessions running unlocks the next ladder step (a "Level up!" moment once, `levelSeen`; Move up swaps like before), under the bottom twice suggests the easier step, 10+ days off starts at about 70% (or one step down) and ramps +3. In Build muscle `ex.target` follows sets × aim (the old one is kept in `endTarget` and comes back with Endurance). Strength (`core/sportStrength.ts`): a set's score is 100 × 1.2^(ladder step) × (1 + reps/30), so a harder move counts as growth; the Strength tab (was Progress) shows "X% stronger than 4 weeks ago", a score per muscle group, best set over time per ladder (one line per step), hard sets this week on a body map (10–20 zone), then the old volume charts and records. Plan (`core/sportProgram.ts`, the Plan tab, settings `planMinutes` 20/30/45, `planPerWeek` 3/4, `planCool`, `planFollow`): 3 full-body A/B or 4 upper/lower sessions, 4–6 exercises × 3 sets, rest 60–120 s (pairs when time is short), 3-min warm-up, optional cool-down, estimated minutes that fit; your own ladder steps are used, missing slots get a library move added as `plan: true, active: false`. Today then shows today's session (rest days say so, with Train anyway; the Rest day button works as before); workout mode follows it: warm-up ring, "Set N of 3 · aim", Set done, a rest ring, next. Celebrations: a full-screen canvas burst, a ring that draws and explodes, rolling stats (vs last time), "New record!" / "Level up!" banners and the Life sound kit (habit, success, levelUp, reward); about 2 s, a tap or Esc skips, none of the motion with reduced motion. Import from Telegram (History card and the top ⋯): pick the Telegram Desktop HTML export(s) of the old sport bot chat; `core/sportTelegramImport.ts` reads it in the browser (bot day cards like "Push-ups (123/117 reps)", "press 0 / 35", your logs like "47pushups"; the highest per exercise per day), a preview lets you untick days, then past days merge into the day log (higher number wins) with one history entry each (`source: 'telegram import'`, `paid: 0`, no sets), badges they earn are noted quietly. `mergeSport` joins `sets` by id, keeps the higher `best`, and `levelSeen`. The sport bot logs sets too ("3x15 push-ups", "push-ups 15 14 12": `parseTelegramText` → `exerciseSets`, `core/sportBot.ts` adds them to Sport Track and replies with the best set and the next level). Points are unchanged: sets, levels, records and imports pay nothing.

## Too easy means harder (2026-11-07)

Pure logic in `core/sportBoost.ts` (tests: `sportBoost.test.ts`, pinned dates), shown through `sportkit.js`. Points unchanged (nothing here pays).
- **Today strip** (`#lv-strip`): the rank ("Trainee → Fighter, 8 pts", big emblem) and one big level badge per exercise ("Push-ups · Level 8 of 15 · Machine", bar to the next level and its requirement); a tap opens Progress → Levels on that ladder. Cards show the level large (`.lv-big`).
- **Automatic harder variation** (`autoVariation`): when a target is above the move's range (e.g. 3×21 at a top of 20), Strength hits 3×8, or a best set says the move is far too easy, Today switches to the hardest variation you could still do at the bottom of its range (about 70% of the reps per step, up to 2 steps, 4 with a best set), banner "Level up: Diamond push-ups 3×8 (your 3×21 push-ups are now your warm-up)" with Undo (the old move is only turned off; Undo sets "Not yet" for 7 days). Once a day per exercise, never mid-workout, not in an easy week.
- **Too easy / Too hard** on every card (`shiftLevel`): a level up (two for a second "Too easy" in 7 days, `state.tooEasy`), the new target set (`state.tune`, today's goal), Undo. A move at the top of its ladder says to slow the tempo (3 s down, pause).
- **Effort** (`state.effort[day][exId]`: Easy / Good / Hard after a finished exercise): easy at the top of the range is enough for the harder move (1 session, not 2) and adds +2 reps next time; hard repeats the target.
- **Best set** (`state.bestSets[family] = { move, reps, day }`, form in the strip and in "Why this level"): counts as 3 working sets of 60% of it on that day and the day before, and a declared easier move counts toward harder levels at 70% of the reps per step (`transferReps`), so 50 regular push-ups is about Level 13, not 8.
- **Strength mode** (`progressMode: 'strength'`, the default since routine v4; Build muscle and Simple totals stay): range 5–8, 4 sets, 150 s rests, a harder move as soon as 3×8 is reached. The harder home moves (one-arm-assisted push-ups, feet-elevated rows, dragon flag) are not in the library yet; at the top of a ladder the card advises slow tempo instead.
- **Thin routine**: 3 moves or fewer shows "A balanced 5–6 move set" (`balancedSet`: push, doorway rows, split squats, plank, hollow hold or a harder finisher) with "Add these N" / "Not now" (7 days).

## Growth coach (2026-11-07)

**Sport now lives in Ben.** The app's bots (webBots, telegramBot) take sport logging: "3x15 push-ups", "push-ups 15 14 12", "8 push-ups +10 kg", "plank 3x45s" and "best set push-ups 50" (`parseSportSay` in `core/sportBot.ts`, read by `botCommands.ts`). Ben replies with a receipt: what was logged, the best set, the level, the next target and the coach's note for next time. A best set is remembered (`state.bestSets`), not logged as a workout. The old separate Python bot is another repo and is left alone.

**The rules, in code** (`core/sportCoachRules.ts`, tests `sportCoachRules.test.ts` with made-up weeks and pinned dates). Every suggestion comes from them and nothing, not even the weekly AI review, may break them:
1. 10-20 hard sets a week per muscle group, on at least 2 days (`muscleLoad`, last 7 days).
2. Sets stop 1-2 reps short of failure (said on every first session and in the card).
3. Progressive overload in a fixed order (`overload`): reps up to the top of the range, then the next harder variation, and at the top of a ladder added weight (5 kg first, then +2 kg), then back to the bottom of the range (5 in Strength mode). Two tough sessions: step back with a slow 3-1-1 tempo.
4. Rest suits the reps (`restFor`: 5-8 reps 2.5-3 min, 9-12 about 1.75, 13-20 about 1.25, holds 1 min).
5. Deload when 6 hard weeks in a row, or 4 with a stall (`deloadCheck`).
6. Protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg and sleep 7+ h come from the Body data (`sportBodyNow` in `main/bodyPlus.ts`, sent to the page with `LIFE_SPORT_INSIGHT`).

**Your coach card** (top of Today, `#your-coach`): the single most important thing this week ("Add 2 sets of pulling: your back is behind"), today's exact session (variation, sets × reps, weight or tempo, rest), one line why, protein/sleep lines, this week's plan. **After every log** the page and the bots call `adjustAfterSession` (`sportAdjust.ts`): progression, step back, swap, +1 set where a muscle is under 10. **Weekly review** (`sportReview.ts`): the page writes the rules' program each Monday (`state.weekProgram`, source `code`); the first training question or "sport review" to Ben lets the strong model (existing `ask` path, kind `weekly`) rewrite it. The output is forced inside the rules (targets 10-20, 2+ sessions, a due deload always on), every number must be in the facts, and `benGround` removes invented times and events; with no model or a bad answer the code's plan stays. **Ask Ben** (`sportAsk.ts`, `isTrainingQuestion`): "why am I not growing?", "is 4×8 archer enough?", rest, deload, protein, answered from his data and the rules.

**Best-set onboarding** (`sportOnboard.ts`): on first open, and from "Re-check my levels" (top menu and the coach card), max reps in one set for push-ups, squats, sit-ups, rows and plank seconds. `placeBest` puts each exercise on the variation where about 10-14 reps is the max, so 4×5-8 is hard (50 push-ups: archer push-ups 4×7). Old exercises are only turned off. **Imported days** (`state.importedDays`, set by `mergeTelegramDays`) are history, streak and volume only: they never raise a level (`isImported` in `sportLevels.ts` / `sportCoach.ts`). **Weekly strength test** (card button when 7 days passed): one max set per main move, saved as real sets flagged `test`; updates levels and the chart, pays no points. **Strength charts**: best set and level over 6 months per ladder, with "+X% in N weeks" from best sets and tests, never totals. **Weighted sets**: type "8 +10 kg" in a card's set box (or "8 push-ups +10 kg" to Ben); sets keep `kg`.

**New moves**: one-arm-assisted push-ups (top of the push-up ladder), pseudo-planche leans (plank ladder, holds), feet-elevated rows and one-arm rows, hollow body rocks, dragon flag negatives, box pistols between Bulgarian and shrimp squats (archer, shrimp, pistol already existed). One photo (box pistol, Free Exercise DB); the rest are drawn. Smoke step "Sport Track onboarding".

## Food counter (2026-11-08)

**What it does.** Body → Food starts with the food counter (above the protein ring). Food photos can be read by AI since 2026-11-09 (see "Food photo logging" below); the copy-prompt way stays: **Copy prompt** (the text is `core/foodPrompt.ts`, one place) → paste it with a meal photo into any free AI app (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) → paste the reply back. `core/foodParse.ts` reads it with plain code (fenced or bare JSON, chatter around it, other field names, "12,5", numbers with units, trailing commas, curly or single quotes, a cut-off reply, plain lines as a last try; missing kcal = 4·protein + 4·carbs + 9·fat). A reply it can't read says so and offers "Copy the prompt again". The draft lists each food: rename, −/+ grams (10 / 25 / 50 g steps; macros scale from the AI's numbers), remove; the AI's question is shown; the day line updates live. Save adds the meal now (type from the time: breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack, changeable). The day picker (last 7 days) adds or fixes meals on a past day; those are marked "added later". "Same as yesterday" and starred meals open in the same draft, so grams can be fixed before Save. Nothing is sent anywhere and **nothing pays points**.

**Goals: slow muscle gain** (2026-11-09; was "lean muscle"; `core/foodGoals.ts`, setting `food_goals`, the Goals sheet): protein 1.8 g/kg (1.6–2.2) × the latest logged weight (no weight: 125 g); kcal = maintenance (Mifflin–St Jeor × activity) **+350** once a weight is logged AND you entered your height and age in Goals (`bodySet`), else a fixed **2,700 kcal**. "Expect about +0.25–0.5 kg a week." Your own kcal or protein goal wins. The g/kg is the one the protein ring uses (`body_plus.gPerKg`). The move runs once per database (`gainDefaultOnce`, flag `food_goals.gainV`; `leanDefaultOnce` is now an alias): the lean 2.0 g/kg becomes 1.8, the lean surpluses +150/+0 become +350/+350, height/age count as entered only when they differ from the app's old defaults (178 cm, 30); nothing else you saved changes. The owner's real height and age are never in code: he types them once in Goals.

**One protein number (no double counting).** A meal's numbers are rows in the one body log: `protein:food` (`<meal id>-p`) and `kcal:food` (`<meal id>-k`). `proteinOfDay` already adds every `protein:*` row, so quick protein logs and food counter meals add up and each is counted once; the protein ring, the counter, the weekly body report and **"why it stalled"** (`stallsOf` → `proteinWeek`) all read the same number with no code change.
