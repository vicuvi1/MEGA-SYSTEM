**Ben quality (BQ, `src/core/benTalk.ts`).** Pure helpers used by `think()` and `brainPrompt()`:

- **The clock in words** (`timeFrame`, `whenWords`): the prompt always gets the clock, the app day (turns at 04:00) and what "tonight", "this morning" and "tomorrow" mean right now. From 00:00 to 04:00 the calendar shown to Ben is the morning ahead, so 07:00 is "in 6 h 29 min, this morning", never "tomorrow morning".

- **No repeating** (`alreadySaid`): the themes, openers and emoji of his last 6 replies go into an "Already said" block (minus what your message brings up), and `tidyReply` takes out "Look, Victor," openers and keeps at most one emoji (none when the last two replies had one). His face comes from `mood`; the reply text no longer starts with a forced face.

- **Messy input** (`fixTypos`): typos of command words ("chagne", "aporve the paln") are fixed before the local parsers; the model gets your words plus "read his message as …". After "no" / "nu" / "нет" the prompt tells him to drop it.

- **Show, don't tell** (`wantsToSee`): asking to see or approve the plan, your day or your tasks always adds the card (`show`: `plan` with ✅ Approve / ✏️ Change, `day`, `tasks`). The day and task cards' items are saved with the reply (`cards` on the coach log entry); Telegram gets them as text.

- **Act, then confirm** (`readBlockEdit`, `matchBlocks`, `pickChoice`): "change/rename/move the deep block …" runs in code: one block fits → done at once (`rename:` / `move:` on a calendar E-block or a waiting plan B-block), the ✅ line is the real result with Undo; several fit → chips "Deep block 1 · 07:00" (one-tap buttons on Telegram), and the next "first one" / "2" / "al doilea" / chip picks it (`pick` on the log entry, 30 minutes). A reply that claims a change with no action gets "Nothing is changed yet" and a "Do it" chip.

- **Routing and time (`src/core/benRoute.ts`, Coach fix).** `routeFor()` reads the kind of message: quick logs and chat → quick model; reviews, goals and advice → strong model with a trimmed day (`trimContext`: a review gets yesterday's numbers from `web/benDigest.ts` up front, not the calendar, PC or plugins; `lean` prompt: 8 facts, 4 turns); "plan my day" → `rhythm.propose()` in code with the plan card; several asks in one message → every action group plus "do all of them". One budget per answer (`THINK_BUDGET_MS` 44 s from the start of `think()`): the strong model may think at most its slot and always leaves `QUICK_RESERVE_MS` for the quick model (the server is told `thinkMs` and `noFallback`, so it doesn't fall back twice); a failed quick try is retried once. If nothing answers, a review comes from the numbers (`reviewFromData`) and anything else returns `{ok:false, retry:true}` with a friendly line: the page keeps your message with "Try again", never the raw error. Extra lookups for `need`: `goals`, `day <date|yesterday>`, `money week`, `sleep7`, `water <from> <to>`, `todoist <project>` (`answerMore`). A review ends with one tap-button (`reviewChange`). Tests: `src/server/benSpeed.test.ts` (slow strong model stub), `benLookups.test.ts`.

- **Speed (where the time goes).** Every answer carries a timing trace (`src/core/benTrace.ts`: each step, each model call with its prompt size, lookup rounds, first words on screen); the server logs it as one `ben <intent> <path> …ms` line. Answered in code, no model: "what should I do now?" (`benQuick`), simple water logs ("log 500 ml water"), "move gym to 18:00" when one block or event fits (`readMoveEdit`). "Plan my day" skips the chat's own Todoist/calendar read (the planner reads them once), shows the packed plan at once and lets the model's block names land in place a moment later (`propose(note, {fastMs})`; `/api/coach` and the Telegram webhook wait for `settle()` after replying; the page stops waiting at the final line). Reviews and goals checks don't read Todoist/calendar; a review shows its numbers as an instant draft that Ben's streamed words replace. Todoist/calendar reads have a timeout and a 45 s copy for follow-ups (any change through Ben forgets it; a slow or failed read uses a copy up to 30 min old). Streamed usage rows are written after the reply (`flushUsage`). When the thinking model runs out of time, the quick answer gets a **Think harder** chip (the same question again, thinking model, up to 30 s). Telegram keeps "typing…" on. Budgets: `benSpeed.test.ts` (realistic latency, 10× faster).
- **Server-side time-outs (2026-11-08, `api/ai.mjs`).** Background asks (the tick's plans, reviews, nudges) give the thinking model at most `BACKGROUND_THINK_MS` (28 s), then the quick model, which gets one retry after 1 s through the fastest provider on a time-out, 429 or 5xx (`quickCall`; chat kinds `chat`/`retry`/`lookup` skip it because `think()` retries itself). A stand-in answer carries `fallback: 'quick'`. Time-outs read "<model> timed out after N s", including a stream that hangs half way. See `docs/areas/ops.md` (AI time-outs).

- **Coach chat fix (2026-11-08): order, one plan card, time and steps, speed.**
  - **True time order** (`src/core/coachOrder.ts`, `orderLog`): the chat is sorted by time when it loads or refreshes; a reply never goes above its question (a server clock a little behind), the answer being written stays last. Your new question and its answer are always the last two messages and the view scrolls there.
  - **One live plan card** (`livePlanAt`): the newest message from today that carries a plan card (a reply's card or the morning plan message) is the live one; every older one is a single line "Plan updated · the newest version is further down". The morning plan message never folds into "N earlier updates". "See the plan" / "Now: Approve" scrolls to that card; when no message shows it yet, Ben posts the plan card as a message at that moment (in time order). The old card pinned under all messages is gone (it was the bug: the morning plan drawn under the 15:21 questions).
  - **Time and steps under each answer:** the muted line now starts with how long it took, tap to last word ("3.2 s · deepseek-v4.1-flash · 11.2k tokens · $0.0004", "0.4 s · Answered from your data · no AI · $0"), always shown. "▸ N steps · reasoning" opens the steps in plain English (`src/core/benSteps.ts`: "Read your sleep log (7 nights)", "The thinking model was slow, so the quick model answered") and, when the thinking model returned reasoning (`api/ai.mjs` reads OpenRouter's `reasoning` and hands it to `opts.onReasoning`), a short summary (`reasoningSummary`: first whole sentences, at most 360 characters). Time, steps and reasoning are kept in `coach_answer_meta` (`ms`, `firstMs`, `steps`, `reasoning`). While Ben works, the seconds count beside his name and the current step reads "Reading your sleep log…"; his words stream in as before.
  - **Answered in code, no model** (`src/core/benAsk.ts`, `askKind`): "how much do I sleep on average" (nights pair the evening bed time with the next wake-up; last 7 nights unless a range is named), "how much have I earned this month" (Finance Helper income + points; unknown data is said to be unknown), "how are my habits", "what are my tasks today" (must-dos, Todoist from the last read or one 1.2 s read, plan; the task card comes with it).
  - **Smaller prompt for short questions** (`askTrim`): a short question (not a log, an edit or a big ask) goes to the quick model with only the context lines it is about (`askKeep`: sleep, money, water, training, focus, habits, tasks, calendar, points) and `lean` memory; Todoist and the calendar are read only for task or calendar questions. "How much did I spend this month?" no longer goes to the thinking model ("month" made it "advice").
  - **Speed** (`src/server/benAskSpeed.test.ts`, made-up life, realistic latency): sleep average 4.8 s → under 0.1 s; earned this month 17.0 s (thinking model) → under 0.1 s; habits 4.8 s → under 0.1 s; tasks today 4.8 s → 0.8 s; "should I go to the gym tonight or rest?" first words 3.2 s → 2.8 s, prompt 7.3k → 4.5k tokens.
- **Grounded answers (Coach fixes, 2026-11-07).** Ben names only what is in his data (`src/core/benGround.ts`, run on every model reply in `think()`): a clock time must be in his context or your message, a meeting / class / lecture / exam must be in the calendar or plan, and "nearly done" needs your own "almost"; a sentence that fails is dropped (all made up: only those parts go). The running timer has its own context line with its real end ("ends 18:15, 4 min left"). In code, no model: "what should I focus on right now?" (`benQuick`: the running timer and its end, the plan block you're in, the real next block or event from today's plan and the calendar Ben read lately), "now I'm going to clean the room" (`benNow.ts`: ▶ Start a timer for it, its plan block's length, and Later; a running timer offers to switch), "I finished X" while X's timer runs (stops and logs it), "just drunk a cup of coffee" (the caffeine log). Plan words (`benPlanText.ts`): the chat line and the plan card share one summary (blocks, focus, must-dos) and the chat line has no version; counts in the model's headline that no longer match are dropped; "create a new plan" while one is approved asks "Replace it?" (Yes re-plans from now keeping done and pinned blocks, No keeps it). Tests: `src/server/benCoachBugs.test.ts` (the five real messages), `src/server/benSelfReview.test.ts` (a 10-message day), `src/core/benNow.test.ts`.
- **Easy to read (Coach fix, v1.4.1379, `src/core/benPlain.ts`).** Every answer leads with the direct answer, uses short whole sentences (no "Coffee in", "Next real thing"), puts 2+ points on their own lines, writes times as "at 15:00 (in 48 min)" (`atIn`), uses no app words (block, window, slot, v2) and ends with one next step (a button when there is an action). The model gets `PLAIN_RULES` and a bad/good example in its prompt; its reply then goes through `plainTidy` (app words out, the known shorthand spelled out, 3+ sentences one per line; planner titles like "Deep block 1" stay). The code answers follow the same rules: "what now?" / "what should I focus on?" (`benQuick`), "what am I doing wrong?", water, streaks, "now I'm going to …" (`benNow`), the coffee log (`coffeePlain`) and the plan lines (`planSummary`: "4 sessions, 3 h 5 min of focus and 3 must-dos"). **"How is my day going?"** is answered in code at once (`asksDaySoFar` → `daySoFar`): a verdict for the time of day, then plan done vs planned, focus vs goal, must-dos, water, what's next, and one step with a button; no Todoist/calendar wait and no model call. Tests: `src/server/benReadable.test.ts` (the real messages with readability checks: sentence length, no shorthand, no app words, answer first).
- **Ben understands you and remembers (LS2–LS6, ME1–ME5, TL1, TL2, TL5).** Pure parts in `src/core/`, the database side in `web/benMind.ts`, wired into `think()`:
  - **Receipt (`benReceipt.ts`):** every reply that did something carries `receipt` ("Added 'Read 10 min' at 21:40 · Calendar ✓ Todoist ✓", several things joined by "; "), built from the real result lines; the Coach feed shows it above the result cards (with "Undo all"), Telegram shows it under the reply with one "↩ Undo all N".
  - **Messy typing (`benNormalize.ts`):** diacritics off, Romanian / Russian / slang command and time words into plain English (`canon`: "fa-mi ziua" → "plan my day", "завтра спортзал в 18" → "tomorrow gym at 18"), his aliases, fuzzy names (`similarity`, `fuzzyFind`, `sureOf`: act on one clear match, ask on two close ones). The original text is tried first; the canonical one is the fallback and what the router reads when the original is plain chat.
  - **Several asks (`benSplit.ts`):** `splitIntents` + `readClause` (add, remind, move, water); when every clause reads, they all run in code with one receipt and one Undo; otherwise the model gets the message as before. A bare "thing + day" typed in Romanian/Russian ("curata camera azi") is an add. An add with a time also books a calendar block (his usual time / duration when he gave one).
  - **One question instead of a guess:** an hour 7–10 with no am/pm today ("at 8") asks with chips 08:00 / 20:00; several blocks fit → chips. The pending pick (`pick` on the log entry) now holds per-option values or action lines and the rest of the message (`rest`), run after the pick. Nothing changes before.
  - **Corrections and memory (`benFacts.ts`, settings `ben_know`):** "X takes 40 min", "gym is always at 18", "by biblio I mean Reading" are saved at once (Undo); the packer's estimates and the router use them. What Ben only guesses (the model's `remember`, "my shift is 17-24") becomes an offer with Yes / No buttons; only Yes saves (a shift also fills My life). "remember that …" is still saved at once. "Forget everything about X" clears facts, preferences, offers, people, goals, unpinned note lines and past-chat points. Only the preferences that fit the message go into the prompt.
  - **Memory page:** Settings → Ben → **Memory** (`BenMemory.tsx`, `life.coach.know`): facts, preferences, habits, goals and people with source and date; edit, delete, add, forget; the waiting offers; the past-chat points.
  - **Real rhythm (`benLearn.ts`, settings `ben_learn`):** each day's plan is kept 30 days; with the focus log and block results it learns a pace multiplier per kind of task (needs 3 samples, 0.6–2.0), the best hours and the usually skipped hours; `packDay` takes `estimate` and `avoid`. **After a long shift** (4 h+) the next hour is quick-tasks-only too (`withRest`, `work.rest`).
  - **Past chats (`benConvo.ts`, settings `ben_convo`):** decisions, struggles and wins found in his messages (at most 150) and a rolling summary; one short matching line goes into the prompt. `life.coach.know.past(q)` is the store for the chat search.
  - **Promises (`benPromise.ts`):** "I'll start the essay tomorrow" (EN/RO/RU) → a check-in in `coach_reminders` with `promise`; the tick asks "You said you'd … today" with Done / Tomorrow / Drop (buttons in Telegram and the app); a promise due today joins that day's plan as one of his asks; the model is told not to add its own check-in.
  - **Smart reminders (`benSmartRemind.ts`):** "after the lab" → that event's end; "after my workout" → when a workout is logged (21:00 at the latest); "when I get home" → the desktop app back after 2+ hours away (20:00 at the latest). The tick arms them (`armSmart`).
  - **Break down (`benBreak.ts`):** "break down X" → 3–6 steps from the quick model (template steps when it can't), each a Todoist task with minutes, a ▶ button for step 1. A big task first due 3+ days ago gets a "Break down …" chip under the day plan (settings `ben_pushbacks`).
  - **Tools (`benTools.ts`):** the audit is at the top of the file; new: `buy: <reward>` (only when he asks to buy/claim, the shop's own rules, Undo) and `myhabit: <name>` (only when he says he did it, pays once, Undo).
