**Ben quality (BQ, `src/core/benTalk.ts`).** Pure helpers used by `think()` and `brainPrompt()`:

- **The clock in words** (`timeFrame`, `whenWords`): the prompt always gets the clock, the app day (turns at 04:00) and what "tonight", "this morning" and "tomorrow" mean right now. From 00:00 to 04:00 the calendar shown to Ben is the morning ahead, so 07:00 is "in 6 h 29 min, this morning", never "tomorrow morning".

- **No repeating** (`alreadySaid`): the themes, openers and emoji of his last 6 replies go into an "Already said" block (minus what your message brings up), and `tidyReply` takes out "Look, Victor," openers and keeps at most one emoji (none when the last two replies had one). His face comes from `mood`; the reply text no longer starts with a forced face.

- **Messy input** (`fixTypos`): typos of command words ("chagne", "aporve the paln") are fixed before the local parsers; the model gets your words plus "read his message as …". After "no" / "nu" / "нет" the prompt tells him to drop it.

- **Show, don't tell** (`wantsToSee`): asking to see or approve the plan, your day or your tasks always adds the card (`show`: `plan` with ✅ Approve / ✏️ Change, `day`, `tasks`). The day and task cards' items are saved with the reply (`cards` on the coach log entry); Telegram gets them as text.

- **Act, then confirm** (`readBlockEdit`, `matchBlocks`, `pickChoice`): "change/rename/move the deep block …" runs in code: one block fits → done at once (`rename:` / `move:` on a calendar E-block or a waiting plan B-block), the ✅ line is the real result with Undo; several fit → chips "Deep block 1 · 07:00" (one-tap buttons on Telegram), and the next "first one" / "2" / "al doilea" / chip picks it (`pick` on the log entry, 30 minutes). A reply that claims a change with no action gets "Nothing is changed yet" and a "Do it" chip.

- **Routing and time (`src/core/benRoute.ts`, Coach fix).** `routeFor()` reads the kind of message: quick logs and chat → quick model; reviews, goals and advice → strong model with a trimmed day (`trimContext`: a review gets yesterday's numbers from `web/benDigest.ts` up front, not the calendar, PC or plugins; `lean` prompt: 8 facts, 4 turns); "plan my day" → `rhythm.propose()` in code with the plan card; several asks in one message → every action group plus "do all of them". One budget per answer (`THINK_BUDGET_MS` 44 s from the start of `think()`): the strong model may think at most its slot and always leaves `QUICK_RESERVE_MS` for the quick model (the server is told `thinkMs` and `noFallback`, so it doesn't fall back twice); a failed quick try is retried once. If nothing answers, a review comes from the numbers (`reviewFromData`) and anything else returns `{ok:false, retry:true}` with a friendly line: the page keeps your message with "Try again", never the raw error. Extra lookups for `need`: `goals`, `day <date|yesterday>`, `money week`, `sleep7`, `water <from> <to>`, `todoist <project>` (`answerMore`). A review ends with one tap-button (`reviewChange`). Tests: `src/server/benSpeed.test.ts` (slow strong model stub), `benLookups.test.ts`.

- **Speed (where the time goes).** Every answer carries a timing trace (`src/core/benTrace.ts`: each step, each model call with its prompt size, lookup rounds, first words on screen); the server logs it as one `ben <intent> <path> …ms` line. Answered in code, no model: "what should I do now?" (`benQuick`), simple water logs ("log 500 ml water"), "move gym to 18:00" when one block or event fits (`readMoveEdit`). "Plan my day" skips the chat's own Todoist/calendar read (the planner reads them once), shows the packed plan at once and lets the model's block names land in place a moment later (`propose(note, {fastMs})`; `/api/coach` and the Telegram webhook wait for `settle()` after replying; the page stops waiting at the final line). Reviews and goals checks don't read Todoist/calendar; a review shows its numbers as an instant draft that Ben's streamed words replace. Todoist/calendar reads have a timeout and a 45 s copy for follow-ups (any change through Ben forgets it; a slow or failed read uses a copy up to 30 min old). Streamed usage rows are written after the reply (`flushUsage`). When the thinking model runs out of time, the quick answer gets a **Think harder** chip (the same question again, thinking model, up to 30 s). Telegram keeps "typing…" on. Budgets: `benSpeed.test.ts` (realistic latency, 10× faster).
- **Server-side time-outs (2026-11-08, `api/ai.mjs`).** Background asks (the tick's plans, reviews, nudges) give the thinking model at most `BACKGROUND_THINK_MS` (28 s), then the quick model, which gets one retry after 1 s through the fastest provider on a time-out, 429 or 5xx (`quickCall`; chat kinds `chat`/`retry`/`lookup` skip it because `think()` retries itself). A stand-in answer carries `fallback: 'quick'`. Time-outs read "<model> timed out after N s", including a stream that hangs half way. See `docs/areas/ops.md` (AI time-outs).

A pasted AI reply (`looksLikeFoodReply`: a JSON block with or without fences and chatter, or plain lines with grams, protein and kcal) is read by `parseFoodReply` → a preview (each food, the total, the AI's question) with −/+25 g per food and ✅ Save / ✏️ Edit grams / ✗ Cancel (`fd|s|<id>`, `fd|e|`, `fd|x|`, `fd|+|<id>|<i>`, `fd|-|`). While the preview is open (2 h), "chicken 175", "type: rice 150" or "1 175" sets that food's grams. Save → `addFoodMeal(…, 'telegram')`, then "Saved: … · today's line" with ↩ Undo (`fd|u|<meal>` = `toggleFoodMeal`). A reply it can't read says so and points to /food. The open preview and the mute live in the synced setting `food_ben`. The route runs first in `handle()` (a cheap check; only a "word number" message reads the setting), after the owner-chat check.
  - **"What should I eat now?"** (also "what to eat", "how much protein left", "ce sa mananc", "что съесть"; `asksFood` → `askKind` kind `food`): answered in code from `whatNextNow` + `foodDayTotals`, in the app chat and on Telegram, no model ("58 g protein left: … .\nSo far: Protein 92 / 150 g · 1,850 / 2,700 kcal today.").
  - **One food push a day** (type `food`, keys `food:evening:<day>` / `food:dinner:<day>`, any `food:` key blocks the other): the **evening nudge** when something was logged after 17:00 and protein is 25 g+ short, from 20:30 (bed − 90 for an early bed) until an hour before bed: "40 g protein short today: a shake + 100 g skyr or 170 g chicken breast fixes it." (the fixes are `whatNextNow`'s ideas); the **dinner reminder** when nothing was logged after 17:00 (also a day with nothing logged), from 21:00 (bed − 60) until 30 min before bed: "Log dinner? Copy the prompt or Same as yesterday." with 📋 Copy the prompt / 🔁 Same as yesterday (yesterday's dinner, else its last meal; a preview on Telegram, saved at once on the island) / 🔕 Mute food nudges for 7 days. Never in a meeting, quiet hours, a paused day or after bed is logged. One channel like the other pushes: the island at the PC (`pushes.foodCheck` / `pushes.food` in `tursoWebBridge.ts`, shown by `benPushFeed.ts`; Copy puts the prompt on the clipboard), else Telegram from the server's tick (`benPushTick.ts`; the food buttons are `fd|p|<key>`, `fd|y|`, `fd|m|`, their answers land in `ben_pushes`). It depends on the tick: with GitHub's throttled timer it can come late (see Known gaps in progress.md).
  - **Sunday food report:** in the same Sunday weekly message, under the body week (sent apart when the message would pass Telegram's 4096 characters; left out when nothing was logged all week): average protein vs goal (days you logged it), average kcal vs goal (days with meals), weight over the week (first → last weigh-in, or the last one before the week), days on the protein goal of 7, one tip chosen from those numbers (`foodReport`).
  - **Meal photos on Telegram (2026-11-09).** Send the bot a meal photo, with an optional caption note ("2 containers, one buckwheat"). The bot is already linked to the owner (only the owner's chat ids, the usual check), so there is no linking code: a stranger's photo is never downloaded or read. The server picks one Telegram size up to 1280 px and 1 MB (`pickPhotoSize`; no resizing), downloads it into memory, and calls the SAME vision read as the website in-process (`foodPhoto` from `api/ai.mjs`, handed in by `api/telegram.mjs` as `env.foodPhoto`; same 50-photos-a-day counter and AI budget), then checks the answer with `core/foodVision.ts` `validateVision` against the saved foods and meals (`savedRefs`). "📸 Reading the photo…" becomes the preview: each food with how many, grams, kcal and protein, "⚠️ not sure, check the portion" on a low-confidence food, the total and the AI's line; buttons −/+ per food (how many; halves under 1), 🔁 Swap (a short list of saved foods, closest names first; `fd|w|`, `fd|W|<draft>|<i>|<n>`, ← Back `fd|b|`), ✅ Save, ✗ Cancel. Typing "eggs 2" sets how many (up to 20), "sauce 60" or "rice 150 g" sets grams. Save logs once through `addFoodMeal` with source 'photo' (the meal id comes from the draft, so a double tap can't log it twice) and replies with `photoReplyLine` from real numbers ("3 Egg + 2 Rye bread: ~376 kcal, 24 g protein. Today: 1,400 / 2,700 kcal, 60 / 130 g. ~1,300 kcal left, 70 g protein; next: your evening and anytime meals cover it (~1,250 kcal)."; the standard-day slots not logged yet when they fill what's left, else the next idea), with ↩ Undo. The daily limit, the budget or an unreadable photo get a plain message and "Or send /food for the copy prompt." The photo is never stored or logged (only the foods you save). A caption with a question ("?") or starting with "Ben" still goes to the coach's brain. Without `env.foodPhoto` (the browser's bot) photos go to the coach as before.
  - **Several photos of one meal on Telegram (2026-11-09, `bots/foodAlbum.ts`).** An **album** (photos sent together: Telegram delivers each as its own update with the same `media_group_id`, about at once, and on Vercel maybe in separate function calls) gets ONE read and ONE preview. The parts meet in the database: each part writes a row `fdp:<chat>:g<group>:<message id>` in `sys_kv` (server only; /api/db refuses sys_*) holding only Telegram's file id (of the size `pickPhotoSize` picks), the caption and the time, never the picture. The first part to write `fdl:<chat>:g<group>` (an atomic insert) is the lead: it says "📸 Reading the photos…", polls the parts every 0.5 s and stops when no new part came for two polls after at least 1.5 s (or 10 parts, or 4 s), marks the group taken, takes and deletes the part rows in one transaction, downloads the photos (first 6; "Only the first 6 photos were read."), calls the same `foodPhoto` once with `images`, and edits its message into the preview ("3 photos read together. …"). The other parts answer nothing; a part that comes after the lead took the group is read on its own. Captions of the parts are joined. **"➕ Add another photo"** sits above Save / Cancel on every photo preview (`fd|a|<draft>`): it opens a 60-second window ("Send the next photo of this meal now… Then tap Done", ✅ Done, read them `fd|r|<draft>` / ✗ Stop adding `fd|q|<draft>`); photos (and album parts) sent in it are rows `fdp:<chat>:a<draft>:…`, each one edits that message ("Got one more photo (2 for this meal)…") and moves the window on; nothing is read until Done. Done reads the preview's earlier photos (their file ids are kept as `fdd:<chat>:<draft>` for this) and the new ones together in ONE call and turns the Done message into the new preview (a new draft; the old preview's buttons close). The earlier photos are not counted again (`foodPhoto(body, {counted})`, set only in-process by the bot; the website can't pass it). Done with no new photo says so and keeps the window; a photo after the window is a new meal. **Kept:** file ids only, deleted right after the read, on Save, Cancel or Stop adding, and by a sweep of every `fd…` row older than 10 minutes on the next photo. Only the owner's chats reach any of this (the usual check runs first; a stranger's album writes nothing and downloads nothing). Tests: `server/foodAlbum.server.test.ts` (album of 3 → one call → one preview, Add another photo → Done → one call with both and `counted: 1`, Stop adding, the window, a stranger, rows gone, a late part, the sweep).
  - **Saved meals in one tap:** `/meals` lists the standard day and your saved meals as buttons (`fd|g|<meal id>` / `fd|g|day`); a tap logs it now (once per button per message, even on a double tap) with ↩ Undo (`fd|v|<id>` undoes every meal of it; kept in `food_ben.undo`). Typing "breakfast", "standard day", "had lunch" or a saved meal's name asks "Log it now?" first (✅ Log it / ✗ No). Source 'saved', no points.
  - **Meal reminders (2026-11-09, `decideMealReminder`, setting Settings → Ben → Pushes → "Meal reminders", `life_fit.pushes.meals`, default on).** The standard day's slots (morning, lunch, afternoon, evening by name, else by meal type; "Anytime" gets none) at breakfast = wake + 45 min (06:00–11:00), lunch 13:00, afternoon 16:30, dinner 19:30. In the 90 minutes after a slot's time (and before the next slot), when nothing was logged for that meal yet (nothing since halfway from the slot before; since 04:00 for the first) and the slot isn't logged: "Lunch time: log your bulgur salad with vegetables + Rye bread?" with ✅ Log it (the slot's usual items; the evening one also offers its swap) / ⏰ 30 min / Skip. 30 min asks once more ("Still time to log lunch…", key `…:again`) unless you logged since. One key per slot a day (`meal:<day>:<kind>`), at most 3 a day (a re-ask counts), never in a meeting, quiet hours, a paused day, after bed or within 30 min of bedtime, and "Mute food nudges 7 days" holds them too. One channel: the island at the PC (`foodPushNow` behind `pushes.foodCheck`, its Log it through `pushes.food('log', key, arg)`), else Telegram from the tick (`fd|l|<key>|<0 or 1>`, `fd|z|<key>`, `fd|k|<key>`; answers land in `ben_pushes`). After the evening reminder the 21:00 dinner reminder doesn't ask again.
