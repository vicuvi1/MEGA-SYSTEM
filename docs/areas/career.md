# Career

> Job board, portfolio, flashcards (SRS), shift study kit, essay planner, career money plan, monthly letter.
> Copied out of [`docs/AI_HANDOFF.md`](../AI_HANDOFF.md) on 2026-11-07; this file is now the maintained copy. Start at [`docs/INDEX.md`](../INDEX.md).

**Career (`pages/Career.tsx`, parts in `career/`, data `web/career.ts`, pure logic `core/career/`).** A sidebar page (after Goals) with tabs Jobs · Portfolio · Cards · Study · Money · Review, kept in synced settings `career_*` (no migration). Jobs (`jobs.ts`): a board found → applied → interview → offer / no (buttons or drag), a weekly application quota with the last 8 weeks, follow-up days (7 after applying, 2 after an interview, 3 after an offer; two chases then it stops). Portfolio (`portfolio.ts`): proof-of-work entries (problem, what I did, skills, link, public or private), drafts from the Cyber path log, export as markdown or a clean standalone page. Cards (`srs.ts`): SM-2 spaced repetition (Again/Hard/Good/Easy, Space and 1–4), 12 new cards a day, cards from "front :: back" lines or drafted by Ben (`/api/ai`). Study: the shift study kit (`studyPack.ts`: a 10–25 min pack of due cards, an essay paragraph, a CTF hint, a reading; Start runs a focus timer named "Study pack", so only the timer pays) and the essay planner (`essays.ts`: due date + words → outline / draft sessions / edit / submit placed backward, Replan, Send to my planner = Todoist tasks with due day and minutes). Money (`money.ts`): emergency fund = N months of the average of the last full months of Finance Helper spending (saved = savings accounts minus the career fund), pay yourself first per income (streak), the career fund ring, the income target by source with your moves, and an educational investing projection (no products, "not financial advice"). Review (`letter.ts`): the monthly career letter written from the numbers. Ben: `careerContext` in his prompt, a career line in the Sunday review, `/quiz` `/pack` `/jobs` and `cr|` buttons on Telegram (`web/bots/careerBot.ts`), and `src/server/careerTick.ts` (one career message a tick, each once a day: the letter in the first week of a month, pay yourself first, a follow-up, an essay behind, the study pack 30 min into the shift). Island: the Study page (`IslandStudy.tsx`, snapshot `study`, action `study`). Screenshots: `CAREER_SHOTS=<dir> node e2e/careerShots.mjs` (`E2E_CAREER_DEMO=1` adds a work window and focus time).
