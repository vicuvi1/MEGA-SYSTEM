# Goals and Season

> Goals of every kind, weekly top 3, the Season page.
> Copied out of [`docs/AI_HANDOFF.md`](../AI_HANDOFF.md) on 2026-11-07, so an agent can read only its own area.
> This file is now the maintained copy for this area: update it, not the big handoff. Start at [`docs/INDEX.md`](../INDEX.md).

- **Weekly top 3.** On Sunday (after the weekly review on Telegram, and on Today → Now on Sunday or Monday) Ben asks "Pick your top 3 for this week" with chips from goals and Todoist projects (`weekPlan.ts top3Chips`, Telegram `w3|` buttons, `week_top3_ask`). The picks are the week plan's big 3 (the same list as Goals → This week's big 3); the rest are saved as `parked` and shown quietly (Today, the island's Day page, Ben's prompt says not to nag about them).

- **Goals of every kind** (`pages/Goals.tsx`, `goalsUi.tsx`, `goalSheet.tsx`, `goalsFx.tsx`; maths in `core/goals.ts` + `core/goalKinds.ts`; service `main/goals.ts`). A goal is a number by a date (`target`), a habit (days a week/month), a do-less `limit`, an every-month `repeat` or a `project` with weighted steps. Goals live in `focus_goals` (kind 'measure') and `goal_checkins` as before; the kind and extras (steps, emoji, colour, drawn cover or a small local photo, "future you" line, a reward link, paused, pinned, own order) are in settings `goal_meta:<id>` (a goal without it is a `target`, so old goals just work). Habits/limits/repeats read live data where the app has it (workouts, reps, water, Waky bedtimes, focus hours, the island's PC time, Finance Helper money, Todoist tasks) or your own taps; Ben's "goalcheck" on them logs the difference. Each card shows the pace line in days ("3 days behind: 30 pushups today gets you back"), today's one step (also in Ben's "Goals now"), the streak 🔥 and the best week; a reward tied to a goal uses Rewards' own lock (`reward_unlocks`, `extras.setUnlock`). Celebrations at 25/50/75/100 % and for each habit week made (`freshMilestones`, seen per device in `ls.goalSeen`; sound switch `ls.goalSound`). Points stay honest: +3 for the first log of a period, +15 per milestone reached by your own check-ins or ticked steps (once each), nothing for numbers that move by themselves. Screenshots: `GOAL_SHOTS=<dir> node e2e/goalShots.mjs` (`E2E_NO_GOALS=1` on the e2e server gives the empty page).

- **The Season page (LF1, LF4–LF7, LF12, LF15, LF18; `pages/Season.tsx`, parts in `saga/`).** One sidebar entry with tabs Season · Vision · Identity · Wins · Adventures · Life wheel. Pure logic, tested in `core/saga.test.ts`: `core/season.ts` (12-week seasons = the goal ladder's quarter, with a wallpaper cover, 6 chapters of 2 weeks (a season saved before 2026-11-09 has no `weeks` and stays 8 weeks with its 8 one-week chapters and its end date), 3 main quests that follow a goal or a tick, the boss: HP = 100 minus main quests 55 + perfect daily-quest days 20 + season XP 15, never below 10 until you say you won: the last blow is the real fight; recap and verdict), `core/identityVotes.ts` (3–5 "I am…" lines; only real ledger earns and your kind-deed wins vote), `core/lifeWheel.ts` (8 areas rated once a quarter, past quarters overlaid, the weakest suggests the next season's theme), `core/sideQuest.ts` (28 offline adventures picked per week by fun money left, the weather plugin's weekend, free evenings from the calendar; the trail map), `core/vision.ts` (word of the year, who I'm becoming, 6–9 cards; goals link a card through `goal_meta.card`), `core/winsWall.ts` (wins collected from the ledger, seasons and adventures plus your own; a low day brings one back), `core/benArc.ts` (Ben's mentor chapters by seasons finished: outfits drawn in `saga/sagaArt.tsx`, his voice and lines; big moments kept in `ben_convo` with `via: 'moment'` by `noteMoment` in `web/benMind.ts`), `core/levelCine.ts` (the 5-second scene for a new rank or a season moment, once across devices in `saga_arc.played`, the synth hit). Data side: `web/sagaStore.ts` (settings `saga_*`, the page view, `sagaContext` lines in Ben's prompt), `web/sagaApi.ts` (`life.saga`). The island's morning card shows the word and today's card, a low day offers "Open your wins", a new rank flashes the island (`islandMoments` kind `rank`). No points anywhere here. Screenshots: `SAGA_SHOTS=<dir> node e2e/sagaShots.mjs` (`E2E_SAGA_DEMO=1`, `e2e/sagaDemo.mjs`); smoke step "Season".

- **The goal ladder (2026-11-09; `core/goalLadder.ts`, `core/ladderPlan.ts`, `web/ladderStore.ts`, `web/ladderApi.ts` = `life.saga.ladder`, `ladder/*.tsx`; synced setting `goal_ladder`).** Quarter → monthly milestones → weekly top 3 → today, connecting Goals, the week plan and the Season instead of a fourth system.
  - **Quarter = the current Season** (12 weeks; an old 8-week one keeps its length, so 2 months). Without a season: 12 weeks from the ladder's own start, rolling on. Months are 4 weeks.
  - **Up to 3 critical goals** from 6 generic templates (no personal numbers in code): Gain about 4 kg lean (5 workouts, protein goal on 6 days, 3 weigh-ins a week; result = weekly average weight from body_comp/body_metrics, start = the last weekly average before the quarter), Get much stronger (planned sessions = your split's training days, 40 hard sets a week; result = push/pull level names, level-ups), Study (10 h focus a week, editable; 4 study sessions on deadline subjects, only scored when a deadline exists), and on "Not now": Earn or save money (money saved a week from your monthly target), Career (3 applications, 1 portfolio piece, flashcards on 5 days), Sleep better (5 nights in bed on time and 7.5 h). A 4th is refused; "Swap in" trades places in one tap; targets and milestones are kept on "Not now".
  - **Scored on actions, never results.** Each action's on-pace share (`done ÷ (target × finished days ÷ 7)`, today not counted until it is over, so Monday reads "New week"); the goal's score is the average; light green ≥ 80, yellow ≥ 50, else red (words: On track / Close / Needs a step). Behind: ONE plain next step for the worst action (`fixLine`), no guilt words. Results are shown on the card but never colour it.
  - **Milestones worked backward** (`suggestMilestones`): an even share of the quarter target per month (+4 kg in 12 weeks → +1.3 / 2.7 / 4 kg); hours and applications = weekly target × weeks. Marked "suggested" until you Confirm or edit (`confirmMilestones`). `milestoneHit`: the number at the month's end, else 3 of 4 weeks scoring 80+.
  - **12-week strip** (`timeline`): each week green/yellow/red, weeks to come outlined.
  - **Week steps → top 3 and the planner.** `ladderTop3`: one step per critical goal, from its action that did worst last week ("5 workouts", "10 h of focus"). The Sunday pick on Today (`WeekTop3.tsx`) and the review use `weekPlan.ladderPrefill` (ladder steps first and picked; your earlier picks this week win). The planner places them as drafts (`ladderPlan.ts`): study = the week's focus hours left as deadline sessions due next Monday, in the room the real deadlines leave (their minutes come off each day first), one per day, stable ids; the morning plan adds today's session (`ladderStudyToday`, called from `rhythmBot.propose`). Workouts = your split's training days left this week (the plan's workout slot already follows the split).
  - **Reviews are app screens, no points.** Sunday review (Sunday, or Monday for last week if missed; other days it looks at this week so far; 2 steps: actions per goal with "Plan it in from Monday: …", then next week's top 3 → saved as the week plan's big 3 + parked, the week marked in `reviews`). Monthly review in the first week of a new month (hit or not, edit next month's milestone). Last week of the quarter: a recap line linking to the Season recap and life wheel, then choose the next 3. Pure data for a later Telegram step: `weeklyReviewData`, `monthlyReviewData`, `quarterLine`.
  - **Today → Now** shows one line (`TodayLadderLine`): the goal that needs it most (else the first) with two actions ("Quarter goal: Gain about 4 kg lean · this week 3/5 workouts, 5/6 protein days"); tap opens Goals → This quarter. Ben's prompt gets `ladderBenLine` (via `sagaContext`). No new pushes.
