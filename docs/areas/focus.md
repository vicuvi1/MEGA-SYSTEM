# Focus boost

> Push ladder, "why I'm stalling" fixes, launch kits, session goal and verdict, workout-only music,
> morning countdown, Dojo mode and win music. Written from commit c8d389e (it had no handoff section).
> Start at [`docs/INDEX.md`](../INDEX.md).

- **Code:** pure logic in `src/core/` and the UI in `src/renderer/src/boost/`. Synced settings are `focus_boost` and `focus_log`. No schema change. Settings live in Settings → Ben → **Focus boost** (three panels: Focus boost, Launch kits, Win music). Tests: `src/core/pushLadder.test.ts` and `src/core/focusBoost.test.ts`.
- **Push ladder** (`pushLadder.ts`, island side in `boost/boostFeed.ts`):
  - Three steps, each with its own sound (Settings → Sound): a soft chime, then a sharp alarm, then a horn plus Ben's voice line plus a Telegram message.
  - It starts on PC-coach drift, or when a focus block started 12+ min ago with no timer. It resets the moment you start.
  - The area push level sets the pace: Soft stops at step 2, Normal climbs every 8 min, Hard every 5. Quiet hours and meetings hold it.
  - The step-3 stake (Ben's 25 min / 50 pts deal) only happens on a tap.
  - An explicit Skip never starts a ladder; it opens the stalling question.
- **Why I'm stalling** (`stallFix.ts`):
  - Shown after "10 min later" or Skip on the push card, the island or Telegram (callback `sl|…`).
  - Fixes: tired → a 10-min light timer · unclear → 5 min to write the next step · too big → Ben's break-down · boring → boring-task music plus the pinned reward · scared → a 5-min start.
  - Reasons are logged. Tired and boring also go to Ben's desk excuses.
- **Launch kits** (`launchKit.ts`, `boost/launch.ts`):
  - Each kit is matched by kind of work or by words in the block title.
  - On desktop, it opens through the checked "workspace" call in `src/desktop/automations.ts`. "Close on end" closes only the apps the kit opened.
  - On the website, kits can only open links, and browser tabs can't be closed.
- **Session goal and verdict** (`sessionGoal.ts`, `boost/FocusBoost.tsx`):
  - When any timer starts, it asks for a one-line goal. At the end it asks Done / Half / Not.
  - The verdict is saved as a block result that `benLearn.ts` reads. Insights shows "Finished work". Points never change.
- **Workout-only music** (`musicOnly.ts`, `boost/musicGate.ts`): a chip per station on the Music page cycles any time / workout only / boring only. The island player pauses marked music outside its timer.
- **Morning countdown** (`morningLaunch.ts`): after the wake-up or the morning brief, the island shows water → push-ups → the first must-do within 2 min, with its kit. Water and push-ups are logged only when tapped.
- **Dojo mode** (`dojo.ts`, `boost/Dojo.tsx`):
  - Three scenes from the wallpaper art, a breathing ink ring timer and an optional rain or hum sound.
  - It ends with a bow. It respects reduced motion, Esc leaves, and the timer keeps running.
- **Win music** (`winMoments.ts`, `boost/wins.ts`):
  - Off by default. Clips are 10–30 s, at most once per 10 min.
  - Never during meetings, quiet hours or music that is already playing.
  - Hooked to level-ups, finished Sport Track workouts and goal milestones.
  - It also listens for `life-win` with kind `season`/`boss`, but the Season page doesn't send that event yet.
- **Telegram plan buttons:** −30/+30 go through `planEdit`. A pinned block answers "📌 pinned, unpin it in the app".

### Study Time (2026-11-10, desktop first; redesign "Study Time 2" the same day)
A time tracker in the style of Focus To-Do: **Plan → Study Time** (sidebar and Ctrl+K). Pure rules in `src/core/studyTime.ts` (blocks, filters, the running timer, stats), `studyPlan.ts` (quick add, tomato dots, the Todoist pull), `studyInsights.ts` (goals, best hours, heat map, timeline, CSV) and `studyNotify.ts` (the notices), all tested with the clock pinned. The database side is `src/main/studyTime.ts` (`StudyService`, tested; outside the bot bundle). The page is `pages/StudyTime.tsx` + `study/` (`TimerPane`, `BlocksPane`, `StatsTab`, `LogTab`); the island part is `studyTimeIslandFeed.ts`.
- **One timer.** A block runs as the focus timer (`focus_running` gets `blockId`, `open`, `pomo`, `pauseKind`, `brkMs`, `breakMin`); a break or a pause is the timer's pause, so it never counts as study and every other timer view keeps working. A second start is refused. The running timer is stored times, so a reload or restart loses nothing.
- **Timer tab: two halves** that fill the window. Left: a big timer whose round ring fills to the planned time (in pomodoro mode: this pomodoro) and turns gold past it; on a break it shows the break's time left in green. **Pomodoro** (your lengths, 25/5 and a 15 min break after 4 by default; `pomo.at` is the studied minutes at which the pomodoro ends, `from` where it began) or **Count up** (the planned length, or an open stopwatch up to 4 h). Buttons with words and tooltips: Pause / Resume (a plain pause, kept apart from breaks), Take 5 (counts the pomodoro, starts its break; a long one after every few) or Break 5/10, Keep going, +5 min, **Switch** (searchable; the time so far is a normal stop for the old block, the new block starts at once in the same mode; under 30 s on the old one logs nothing), **Stop** asks "What did you do?" (one line, `study_log.n`). Timer & sounds: the lengths, the end and break-over sounds (made by the sound kit), white noise / soft noise / rain and a soft tick while studying (the island plays them, only while you study), the gentle notices switch. Under it: today's sessions with times and notes (add or change a note later; it changes no minutes or points).
- **Right half:** the quick add bar (`parseQuickAdd`: "Math lab 4 50m #practice tomorrow" → name, length 50, tag, day; also `1h30`, `3p` pomodoros, `@subject`, weekdays, `next week`, `in 3 days`, a date, `daily` / `weekdays` / `weekly` / `every monday`, `*` quick; an existing subject named in the text is used). Filters Today (no day, today or late), Tomorrow, This week, All, Completed, then each subject and tag with **this week's** time and a "This week" total; sessions with no block (a plain focus timer, older sessions) take the block of the same name, else count as **No block**, so the chips always add up to the total. Blocks: subject colour and emoji, tomato dots (estimated pomodoros against done: counted pomodoros, else whole pomodoros of the minutes; a repeating block counts since it was last done), due dates (red when late), repeat (Done moves it to its next day), drag to reorder (or Alt + arrows; `reorder` keeps the other blocks in place), Start (or Switch while one runs), Done, Edit.
- **Todoist:** pick study projects or labels (✓ Todoist); matching open tasks come in as linked blocks (`todoistBlocks`: section or project as the subject, labels as tags, duration as the length, its day), follow their task's name and day, are done here when done or deleted in Todoist, and a recurring task that comes back opens its block again. Done on a linked block completes the task through the existing Tasks path (`life.todoist.complete`), never a second Todoist client. Read with `life.todoist.everything`, at most every 2 minutes and on "Pull now".
- **Blocks and prefs** (synced setting `study_blocks`): the blocks in your order, subject colours and emoji, weekly goals per subject, and the prefs (mode, lengths, sounds, noise, tick, gentle notices, Todoist rule). An empty store gets three made-up starter blocks once.
- **Sessions** stay `focus_sessions` rows (Insights, Ben and the week report see them). Study Time's part per session is the synced setting `study_log`, keyed by the session's start time: block, break and pause minutes, pomodoros, note, source `timer` / `manual` / `fixed` (at most 3000, 400 days). No schema change.
- **Caps and the overnight rule:** a stop counts at most planned + 30 min (pomodoro: the planned length follows the pomodoro), never past 4 h. A timer that ran past that cap, into a new app day (04:00) with over an hour on it, or paused for over 3 h, shows "Still running since …?": you give the real end time (saved on the day it began, capped, **no points**) or drop it.
- **Points:** a normal Stop or Switch pays like any focus timer (live time). Typed-in sessions and a forgotten timer put right pay nothing; changing the minutes or day of a paid session gives its points back; removing a session takes back what it paid; a note changes nothing.
- **Stats** (the full window, 1280–1920): Day / Week / Month / Custom; KPIs (studied and the change, sessions, breaks, streak, against plan, best day); a bigger donut by subject / tag / block with a legend; tap a slice for its sessions and an 8-week trend, else **goals this week** (bar, an even-pace mark, "⚠ Behind: about N min a day gets you there", 🎉 when reached; set "Math 6 h"); a 52-week heat map (levels by your own quartiles); best hours (`bestHours`: minutes per clock hour from timed sessions, the 3 hours whose sessions run longest; `life.study.bestHours()` for the planner and Ben); the day's timeline (06:00–24:00, wider when needed, ‹ › days); **Export CSV** (block, category, tags, start, end, minutes, break, pause, pomodoros, source, note).
- **Notices** (`studyNotice`, one at a time, once each): a forgotten timer → break over ("Back to Math", +5 min break, Stop) → pomodoro done ("Take 5" / "Keep going" / Stop; long break after every few) then one over-time nudge 10 min later → planned time reached (Stop / +5 / Keep going) then one nudge 15 min over → with nothing running: a goal reached (once a week each), a due or late block waiting (no study for 45 min, 09:00–22:00; Start / In an hour / Not today), a goal behind (14:00–21:00, once a day). Timer notices ring your end or break sound; in a meeting they come as a dot and the gentle ones wait. On the desktop the island shows them; the app window only shows a short note (`StudyAlarm`, which checks every 2 s only while a study block runs, to keep idle server calls low) and, with no island, plays the sound.
- On the website the page works the same on the same data; the in-app timer bar never dims the Study Time page.
