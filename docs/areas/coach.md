# The coach (Coach today, weekly review, safety rules)

> One coach that does the thinking: what to do today, how it went, and what changes next week.
> The coach is **Ben**: no second assistant. It reuses food (goals, standard day, the one calorie check),
> training (split, sessions, roadmap, warnings), body (body_comp weekly averages) and the goal ladder.
> Start at [`docs/INDEX.md`](../INDEX.md). Added 2026-11-09.

## Pure core (`src/core/`, tests `coachCore.test.ts`, made-up data, pinned clock)
- **`coachConfig.ts`**: the synced setting `coach_config` (`cleanCoach`). Generic defaults only: slow gain +0.25–0.5 kg a week, 4 meals (used only without a standard day), home training at most 30 min in the morning, 6–12 reps at 1–3 reps in reserve, the morning routine (banana + milk → wait 20–30 min → warm-up → workout → shower → big breakfast), bed by 00:00, 7.5–8 h, English or Romanian, AI line on, blood test dismissed on. The owner's numbers (food goal, standard day, split) come from his own settings at run time.
  - **Ramp-up** (`rampKcal`): with a start day and a baseline, week 1 = baseline + half the gap to the goal, week 2 = the goal; never under 2,200. `baselineFrom` = the average of logged days (> 300 kcal) in the 14 days before the start, else 1,500. "Start ramp-up today" in the settings sheet.
- **`coachPlan.ts`** `dailyPlan(inputs, date)`: deterministic.
  - Training from `trainWeek.todayPlan` (the 48 h rule and missed sessions: a missed session in the last 2 days moves onto a rest day when it keeps 48 h). Session: its moves at your level with "Last time … → aim to beat it", minutes capped by the config. Rest: "Rest day: walk 20 min, eat everything." Done: done for today.
  - Food: kcal = the food counter's goal (accepted calorie changes included) through the ramp-up and the floor; meals = the standard day's slots; extras = easy add-ons (milk, peanut butter, banana, oats) that fill the gap from the standard day to the goal; `change` = the newest accepted change in the last 7 days ("+200 kcal this week: 1 glass of milk + 1 spoon of peanut butter a day").
  - Morning routine (warm-up and workout left out on a non-training day), one focus/tip key, notes (low intake, blood test), checks: workout, each meal, weigh-in, bed.
  - **Ticks** (`ticksOf`): real logs first (a session or sets today or a workout row; a meal logged with that slot, else a free slot of the same meal type; a weigh-in in body_comp or body_metrics; bed from the sleep log, known the next day). Your own tick only for meals and bed; a workout or weigh-in ticks only from a real log. **Ticks pay nothing.**
  - The day store `coach_days` (`cleanDays`): last 120 days, under 120,000 characters (oldest go first): the day's short record (`snap`, written only when it changes), your own ticks, the day's line, the check-in.
- **`coachText.ts`**: the day's one line + one tip. The AI is asked once a morning through `/api/ai` (the usual budget) with a coach prompt (friendly, short, practical, ≤ 80 words, one action, the plan as data, JSON answer); `parseAiLine` keeps it only when every number is in the plan (the benGround rule), it is short, and every sentence passes the safety rules. Otherwise the template (`templateLine`, EN/RO). A template is tried again with the AI after 2 h (2 tries); a changed plan (`planKey`: training + kcal) gets one more try, and a kept AI line is shown only while the plan is the same.
- **`coachSafety.ts`** (hard rules, never left to the AI): 2,200 kcal floor; no cut while the weekly-average weight isn't rising (≥ +0.1 kg); < 1,600 kcal on 3+ of the last 7 logged days → a gentle note with easy add-ons; no body put-downs, no fat burners, no "eat less" unless a cut is allowed, no goal under the floor (`safeText` drops the sentence); red-flag words EN + RO (dizziness, chest pain, fainting, sharp or joint pain, breathing, heart racing; a plain "no dizziness" and normal soreness don't count) → "Stop training and see a doctor", no exercise advice; blood test (hemogram, ferritin, vitamin D, B12) from the start until dismissed, then every 30 days; supplements: optional protein shake, optional creatine 3–5 g.
- **`coachWeek.ts`** `weeklyReview`: weekly average weight vs last week, waist change, average kcal and protein, sessions done vs planned, level-ups, check-in energy and soreness; decisions: calories (the one calorie check `foodAdjust`, gated by the floor and "no cut unless rising"; a held-back cut becomes a calm note), level-ups (the roadmap's own rule), a lighter week (the tired warning, or very sore on 3+ check-ins); the summary line ("Week 3: +0.3 kg, 5/5 workouts, archer push-ups unlocked. Keep going."). Saved in `coach_weeks` (26 weeks: summary, applied, skipped). The week: Sunday → this week, Monday → last week, other days this week so far.

## Data side and API (`src/renderer/src/coach/`)
- `coachStore.ts` reads the plan's inputs: Sport Track's state (the page passes its own copy, the freshest; else the synced one), the food goal (`foodTargetsNow`), the standard day, the calorie history, logged kcal, body_comp/body_metrics, workouts.
- `coachApi.ts` = **`life.coach.today`**: `view(st)`, `line(st)`, `tick(id)`, `logMeal(slotId)` (the slot's usual items through `addFoodMeal`, source 'saved', with its slot; Undo = `undoMeal(ids)`), `config()`, `saveConfig(patch)`, `startRamp(baseline?)`, `dismissBlood()`, `week(st)`, `decide(id, apply, st)` (calories go through `answerAdjust`; a level-up or lighter week is written first by the page with the roadmap's `setLevel` / the warnings' `setLighter` + the usual dose, then recorded).
- Step 2 adds `checkinView(st)`, `checkinSave({energy, soreness, sleep}, st)`, `proposal(op, id)`, `nudgePrefs()` / `setNudgePrefs(patch)` (below). The older `checkin`, `checkinData`, `context`, `redFlag`, `nudges` stay.

## The coach talks (step 2, 2026-11-09): `src/core/coachTalk.ts` (pure, tests `coachTalk.test.ts`), `coach/coachTalkStore.ts` (database), tests `src/server/coachTalk.server.test.ts`
- **Evening check-in.** The facts come from the logs (`checkinFacts`): kcal and protein eaten vs the day's goal, the workout (done / not done / rest day), the weigh-in (body_comp, else body_metrics), last night's sleep (`nightsFrom` of the sleep log), the low-intake note, tomorrow's session, bed time, language. Then 3 taps: energy 1–5, soreness 1–5, sleep (6–9 h plus the logged hours, prefilled). Saved in `coach_days` (`checkin`: energy, soreness, sleep, reply). The reply is **decided by rules** (`checkinReply`: what went well = the workout, protein ≥ 90 %, kcal ≥ 90 %, the weigh-in, a fed rest day, good sleep; one thing for tomorrow = the gentle add-ons after low intake, add-ons to reach the goal (never under 2,200), protein at every meal, lights out (short sleep or low energy), an easy pace (very sore before a session), beat last time by one rep, or the rest-day line). The AI only rephrases it (`checkinPrompt`, kind `nudge`, so the fixed text when the budget is used up); `parseCheckinAi` keeps it only as JSON, ≤ 2 sentences, ≤ 50 words, the same numbers and every sentence safe; else the rule text. EN and RO.
  - Where: Telegram push at about 21:00 (earlier for an early bed, not before 20:00; until 23:30 or 10 min before bed) with buttons `ck|e|<1–5>` → `ck|s|<e><s>` → `ck|h|<e><s>|<half hours or x>` (`web/bots/coachBot.ts`, the message is edited at each step; a tap after it is done says so); the island card at the PC ("✍️ Check in" opens Today, "Not tonight"); and a card under the coach card on Today → Now and Body → Today after 20:30 (`CoachCheckin.tsx`). With the coach check-in on, Ben's old 20:00 mood check-in on Telegram stays quiet (`telegramBot.ts`).
  - The weekly review averages energy, soreness and now sleep from the check-ins (`coachWeek.ts`).
- **Gentle nudges** (`decideCoachNudge`, type `coach`, keys `coach:<kind>:<day>`, one each a day; never in a meeting, quiet hours, a paused day or after bed; the one channel: island at the PC through `pushes.foodCheck` → `foodPushNow`, else Telegram from the tick `benPushTick.ts`):
  - morning, from the wake time for 2 h: "Upper body today 💪 Banana + a glass of milk first, then wait 20–30 min." (the routine's first two steps, only for a morning session; a rest day gets the rest line; not once trained);
  - protein, 14:00–20:00 when under 40 % of the goal: "Protein's behind (40 / 130 g): <two saved foods richest in protein> with your next meal.";
  - the evening check-in (above).
  - Switches: `life_fit.pushes.coachMorning / coachProtein / coachCheckin` (default on), in the coach settings sheet and in Settings → Ben → Pushes (one row "Coach: Morning · Protein · Check-in").
  - **One cap of 3 a day** for coach nudges + meal reminders + the evening food push (`countsToCap`: keys `coach:`, `meal:`, `food:`; a meal re-ask counts). **Priority: check-in > protein > morning > meal reminders and food pushes.** A lower one may go only when the ones above it that can still come today keep their place (`reservedFor`): the check-in until its window ends (on, not done, not sent); protein from 11:00 to 20:00 while it is behind. So with every coach nudge on, at most one meal reminder fits on most days; switching the morning nudge off frees a place. In the tick and on the island the coach nudge is decided first, then `foodCapAllows` gates the meal reminder and the food push.
- **Ask the coach** (Ben's chat in the app and on Telegram, `webBots.ts think()`):
  - red-flag words (`redFlag`, EN + RO) → ONLY "Stop training and see a doctor" in his language, before any model call or other fast path;
  - "I missed Tuesday, what now?" (`missedAsk` / `missedAnswer`, EN + RO) in code from the week and the training log: catch it up today when it keeps 48 hours, else do today's session without doubling up, or rest and carry on;
  - a food, training or body question (`isCoachAsk`) gets `coachNote`: the hard rules (language of his message, ≤ 80 words unless he asks for detail, one action, never under 2,200 or a cut/deficit while the weekly weight isn't rising, no body talk, only an optional shake or creatine 3–5 g, red flags), whether the weight is rising, `coachContext` (config, last 7 days, today's plan), the saved foods and standard day, and the proposal format. About 2 KB, only on those questions;
  - the reply is checked in code (`guardReply`): a sentence under the floor, a cut or deficit while not rising, body talk, fat burners or pills is taken out; nothing left → today's plan line;
  - **proposals**: the model may add `propose` (swap a food in a slot, a new meal for a slot, move a training day, start or stop the ramp-up). `readProposal` checks it against his data (saved foods and slots only, a real training day, never a food change that lowers the standard day while the weight isn't rising or takes it under 2,200) and stores it in the synced setting `coach_props` (12, 7 days). The reply gets "💡 <the change>" and ✅ Apply / Not now (`coach:apply:<id>` / `coach:skip:<id>`; Telegram `cp|a|<id>` / `cp|n|<id>`). Nothing changes before Apply. Apply goes through the existing setters (`saveFoodSaved`, Sport Track's `trainWeek` through `saveSport` with the 48-hour rule, `coach_config` ramp) and keeps what it replaced; ↩ Undo (`cp|u|<id>`, or the app's Undo button) puts it back. The model can't make an Apply button itself (`readButtons` drops them).
- Cost: the check-in phrasing is one short call per check-in; the coach note goes only with coach questions; red flags and missed sessions use no model.
