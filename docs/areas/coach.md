# The coach (Coach today, weekly review, safety rules)

> One coach that does the thinking: what to do today, how it went, and what changes next week.
> The coach is **Ben**: no second assistant. It reuses food (goals, standard day, the one calorie check),
> training (split, sessions, roadmap, warnings), body (body_comp weekly averages) and the goal ladder.
> Start at [`docs/INDEX.md`](../INDEX.md). Added 2026-11-09.

## Pure core (`src/core/`, tests `coachCore.test.ts`, made-up data, pinned clock)
- **`coachConfig.ts`**: the synced setting `coach_config` (`cleanCoach`). Generic defaults only: slow gain +0.25–0.5 kg a week, 4 meals (used only without a standard day), home training at most 30 min in the morning, 6–12 reps at 1–3 reps in reserve, the morning routine (banana + milk → wait 20–30 min → warm-up → workout → shower → big breakfast), bed by 00:00, 7.5–8 h, English or Romanian, AI line on, blood test dismissed on. The owner's numbers (food goal, standard day, split) come from his own settings at run time.
  - **Ramp-up** (`rampKcal`): with a start day and a baseline, week 1 = baseline + half the gap to the goal, week 2 = the goal; never under 2,200. `baselineFrom` = the average of logged days (> 300 kcal) in the 14 days before the start, else 1,500. "Start ramp-up today" in the settings sheet.
- **`coachPlan.ts`** `dailyPlan(inputs, date)`: deterministic.
  - Training from `trainWeek.todayPlan` (the 48 h rule and missed sessions: a missed session in the last 2 days moves onto a rest day when it keeps 48 h). Session: its moves at your level with "Last time … → aim to beat it", minutes capped by the config. Rest: "Rest day: walk 20 min, eat everything." Done: done for today.
  - Food: kcal = the food counter's goal (accepted calorie changes included) through the ramp-up and the floor; meals = the standard day's slots; extras = easy add-ons (milk, peanut butter, banana, oats) that fill the gap from the standard day to the goal; `change` = the newest accepted change in the last 7 days ("+200 kcal this week: 1 glass of milk + 1 spoon of peanut butter a day").
  - Morning routine (warm-up and workout left out on a non-training day), one focus/tip key, notes (low intake, blood test), checks: workout, each meal, weigh-in, bed.
  - **Ticks** (`ticksOf`): real logs first (a session or sets today or a workout row; a meal logged with that slot, else a free slot of the same meal type; a weigh-in in body_comp or body_metrics; bed from the sleep log, known the next day). Your own tick only for meals and bed; a workout or weigh-in ticks only from a real log. **Ticks pay nothing.**
  - The day store `coach_days` (`cleanDays`): last 120 days, under 120,000 characters (oldest go first): the day's short record (`snap`, written only when it changes), your own ticks, the day's line, the check-in.
- **`coachText.ts`**: the day's one line + one tip. The AI is asked once a morning through `/api/ai` (the usual budget) with a coach prompt (friendly, short, practical, ≤ 80 words, one action, the plan as data, JSON answer); `parseAiLine` keeps it only when every number is in the plan (the benGround rule), it is short, and every sentence passes the safety rules. Otherwise the template (`templateLine`, EN/RO). A template is tried again with the AI after 2 h (2 tries); a changed plan (`planKey`: training + kcal) gets one more try, and a kept AI line is shown only while the plan is the same.
- **`coachSafety.ts`** (hard rules, never left to the AI): 2,200 kcal floor; no cut while the weekly-average weight isn't rising (≥ +0.1 kg); < 1,600 kcal on 3+ of the last 7 logged days → a gentle note with easy add-ons; no body put-downs, no fat burners, no "eat less" unless a cut is allowed, no goal under the floor (`safeText` drops the sentence); red-flag words EN + RO (dizziness, chest pain, fainting, sharp or joint pain, breathing, heart racing; a plain "no dizziness" and normal soreness don't count) → "Stop training and see a doctor", no exercise advice; blood test (hemogram, ferritin, vitamin D, B12) from the start until dismissed, then every 30 days; supplements: optional protein shake, optional creatine 3–5 g.
- **`coachWeek.ts`** `weeklyReview`: weekly average weight vs last week, waist change, average kcal and protein, sessions done vs planned, level-ups, check-in energy and soreness; decisions: calories (the one calorie check `foodAdjust`, gated by the floor and "no cut unless rising"; a held-back cut becomes a calm note), level-ups (the roadmap's own rule), a lighter week (the tired warning, or very sore on 3+ check-ins); the summary line ("Week 3: +0.3 kg, 5/5 workouts, archer push-ups unlocked. Keep going."). Saved in `coach_weeks` (26 weeks: summary, applied, skipped). The week: Sunday → this week, Monday → last week, other days this week so far.

## Data side and API (`src/renderer/src/coach/`)
- `coachStore.ts` reads the plan's inputs: Sport Track's state (the page passes its own copy, the freshest; else the synced one), the food goal (`foodTargetsNow`), the standard day, the calorie history, logged kcal, body_comp/body_metrics, workouts.
- `coachApi.ts` = **`life.coach.today`**: `view(st)`, `line(st)`, `tick(id)`, `logMeal(slotId)` (the slot's usual items through `addFoodMeal`, source 'saved', with its slot; Undo = `undoMeal(ids)`), `config()`, `saveConfig(patch)`, `startRamp(baseline?)`, `dismissBlood()`, `week(st)`, `decide(id, apply, st)` (calories go through `answerAdjust`; a level-up or lighter week is written first by the page with the roadmap's `setLevel` / the warnings' `setLighter` + the usual dose, then recorded).
- **For the next step (check-in, nudges, chat):** `checkin({energy, soreness, note}, date?)`, `checkinData(date?)`, `context(st)` (= `coachContext`: config + last 7 days + today's plan), `redFlag(text)`, `nudges(now, sent)` (= `nudgeConditions`: morning 06–10, protein < 40 % from 14:00 to 20:00, evening check-in 20:30–23:30). All pure functions are exported from `src/core/coach*.ts`.

## Screens
- **Coach today** (`CoachToday.tsx`, `coach.css`, tokens only, reduced motion stops the fade):
  - Today → Now: one slim strip under the top line (training + Start, the next meal to tick with "n/5", weigh-in, bed, ⌄ opens the card in place). The Workout habit is folded out of Now's habits while the coach is there (`DayFlow fold`), so the workout shows once. Now stays about 1.6 screens.
  - Body → Today: the full card at the top (the moves stay in the workout card below it). Two columns on a wide card (training, ticks, routine, tip | food), one on a phone.
  - A meal tick logs the usual meal for real (toast with Undo); "Ate other" is your own tick; the weigh-in opens Body → Today; Start opens Body → Training's session.
- **Coach settings** (`CoachSettings.tsx`, ⚙ on the card): goal band, ramp-up (baseline, start today), meals, training minutes, time, reps and reps in reserve, morning routine steps, bed time and hours, language, AI line.
- **One weekly review** (`WeeklyReview.tsx`, `CoachWeek.tsx`): "Weekly review" on the card opens the goal ladder's Sunday review, whose first step is now "Body and training" (`CoachWeekStep`), then the goal actions, then next week's top 3 (3 steps; 2 without the coach). No points.
- Smoke step "Coach today" (strip, open, moves, meals, routine, line and tip, a meal tick logged with its slot and Undo, "Ate other", bed, no points, the review's first step, a level-up applied, Body → Today shows the same ticks). `COACH_SHOTS=<dir>` saves `coach-today-phone.png` and `coach-week-desktop.png`.
