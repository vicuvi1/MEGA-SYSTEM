# Music

> The SoundCloud player, stations, Your SoundCloud.
> Copied out of [`docs/AI_HANDOFF.md`](../AI_HANDOFF.md) on 2026-11-07, so an agent can read only its own area.
> This file is now the maintained copy for this area: update it, not the big handoff. Start at [`docs/INDEX.md`](../INDEX.md).

- **Music (MU).** One SoundCloud player per PC: `src/renderer/src/music/engine.ts` (the Widget API, queue, shuffle / repeat, sleep timer with a fade, recently played in localStorage `ls.musicRecent`). On the desktop it lives in the island window (`ScHost` in `IslandMusic.tsx`); the app window's Music page (`pages/Music.tsx`) drives it over the BroadcastChannel `life-music` (`music/remote.ts`); on the website, or with no island answering, it lives in the page (`musicHost.tsx`, mounted in App). The island's Music tab is `IslandMusicTab.tsx` (header ♫, the `music` tile kind, the media row); the pill shows the artwork in a progress ring, an equalizer and (optional) the lyric line; the peek has a mini player. Synced lyrics come from LRCLIB and covers for PC players from the iTunes search (`core/lyrics.ts`); the real waveform from `wave.sndcdn.com`; the colour from a 24×24 canvas sample of the artwork (accent if blocked). Several players: SoundCloud plus every Windows media session (`mediaList` in `core/pcMedia.ts`, pcx op `mediaList`, asked every 3 s only while a Music view is open), with a pin. Synced options in `island_kit.music` (`core/music.ts`: stations, favourites, focus mix, lyrics, lyric on the pill, equalizer mode, pin, "Ben can see what's playing", off by default, which writes `music_now` for `lifeContext()`). The media keys are registered only while the player is loaded (`islandKit` op `mediaKeys`). The equalizer and the "breathing" artwork are drawn, not measured: the audio plays in SoundCloud's cross-origin frame. CSP `connect-src` allows `wave.sndcdn.com`, `lrclib.net`, `itunes.apple.com`. **Starter stations and vibes:** `core/starterStations.ts` lists 8 public SoundCloud label/curator profiles (Lofi beats, Deep focus, Chill electronic, Jazz cafe, Ambient drift, Upbeat workout, Classical study, Evening soul) shown as "Pick a vibe" cards (`music/VibeGrid.tsx`, generated gradient covers) with "Play something for now" (time of day, focus, workout); hidden ones sync in `island_kit.music.hiddenStarters`; one SoundCloud won't play here (error, or no track shown) is marked unavailable on this device (`music/stationHealth.ts`, `ls.musicUnavail`, retried after a week). While it plays the island's panel glows and the pill's equalizer pulses with a simulated beat (`music/IslandMusicFx.tsx`; SoundCloud's audio can't be analysed), and a small "now playing" card shows on the pill when the track changes (`music.nowToast`). **Your SoundCloud (two ways in):** (1) *Connect by username* (website and desktop): you type your public profile name (or paste its link); only that name is kept, synced in `island_kit.music.scUser`. It shows a "Your SoundCloud" row at the top of the Music page and the island's Music page: Your likes (`/<name>/likes`), Your tracks and your public playlists, played in the embed player. The playlists are asked from the embed player (`/<name>/sets`, `getSounds`) in a hidden frame, at most once a day and only while the row shows, cached in `ls.scUserSets`. (2) *Sign in to SoundCloud* (desktop only, off until you press it): SoundCloud's real site in a `WebContentsView` owned by the main process (`src/desktop/scWeb.ts`, IPC channel `scweb`, state pushed as `scweb:state`), in its own saved session `persist:soundcloud` with no preload, no Node, context isolation and the sandbox on, every permission refused, downloads refused. Its main frame may go only to soundcloud.com, sndcdn.com (and subdomains) and the Google / Apple / Facebook sign-in pages; other links open in the system browser. The Music page's "Your SoundCloud" tab lays the view over its panel (compact zoom, Back, Home, Sign out = clear the whole session); leaving the tab only detaches it, so music keeps playing. While the view exists a small script reads `navigator.mediaSession` and the player bar (1 s while playing or shown, else 5 s); buttons click SoundCloud's own player buttons (its keyboard shortcuts as the fallback). It is the player "SoundCloud (signed in)" (`scweb`) in the player list (pin, pill, peek, lyrics), follows the same pause rules (call, lock, focus) and never plays at the same time as our own player. The island adds "Your mixes" (the "Mixed for …" tiles read from the home page; empty and linking to the tab if SoundCloud's page changes) and a ♥ that presses SoundCloud's like and checks it changed. Pure checks in `core/scAccount.ts` (name parsing, where the view may go, now-playing mapping, tile validation). Page side: `music/scWeb.ts`, `music/YourSoundCloud.tsx`. No CSP change was needed (the view is not a frame of our page).
