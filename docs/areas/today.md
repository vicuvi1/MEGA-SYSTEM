### Pages and features


Today, Calendar, Tasks (Todoist), Goals, Focus, Progress, Reviews (month/year), Coach, Sleep (Waky embedded), **Discipline** (one built-in page; MAN is no longer embedded), Money (Finance Helper embedded), Body (Sport Track embedded), Rewards, **Notebook** (`pages/Notebook.tsx`, synced `notes` table; Ben can write notes too), Settings. Embedded apps live in `src/renderer/public/apps/` and talk to the host by `postMessage` (`LIFE_SYSTEM_BRIDGE`).

**Today → Now: Top 3, Other tasks, habits (a simple to-do list and tracker).** Top: one slim line (`TodayHero slim`): Ben's face, what's on now, "Next: <event> 13:30 in 3h", "Plan not approved [✓ Approve]" when Ben's plan waits, the reward you're saving for, and a ⋯ menu with Plan my day (`life-plan-day`), Shut down my day (`life-close-day`) and Life mode… (`life-mode-open`; `LifeModeCard quiet` shows nothing on a normal day). No score/focus/must-dos tiles and no separate NEXT strip on Now. Below, on a wide screen (the Now column at least 700 px) two cards side by side, stacked on a laptop or phone: **Top 3** (`habits/MustDos.tsx`) are the day plan's must-dos (the same list Ben, the island and Telegram use): big checks, type to add (up to 3), drag to reorder (`focus.setPlan` keeps done ones done), ★ takes one out (unstar in Todoist, or removes a typed one), a tick strikes the line and pops "✓ +10"; the first 3 pay +10 as before, a ⭐ Todoist task is completed in Todoist too; the week's big 3 is one small line in its header. **Other tasks** (`dayflow/OtherTasks.tsx`): the week's open big 3 ("This week", +25) then Todoist's due today and overdue in one list (overdue marked softly, time · length · area as small text), a circle completes it through the usual path (`life.todoist.complete`), ☆ promotes it to the Top 3, "Add a task…" adds it to Todoist for today, more than 8 fold into "Show N more"; full editing stays on the Tasks tab. **Habits** (`habits/HabitBoard.tsx`): one row per habit (icon, name, what's left or when it's due, last 7 days as dots, the streak, a circle to tick it), or a compact grid (⋯ → Show as a grid, per device `ls.hbView`); no Morning/Day/Evening boxes (one list: your order, else morning → evening, done ones last, `core/habitBoard.ts listTiles`). Auto habits (Workout from Sport Track, Focus from the timer, Log spending from Finance) show live progress ("0/50 min") and a tap only opens that app or timer: a tap never pays for something that didn't happen; the rules stay in `dayflow/hooks.ts` (`useHabits`) and the services. Long-press or a row's ⋯: skip today (no points, the streak waits), undo today (your own habits), details (28 days), edit, earlier / later (or drag), hide. The header: "Habits N of M", a thin bar, good days in a row, the money pill ("51 lei left today + Add") and ⋯ (add a habit, list/grid, keep the order fixed, hidden ones); "+ Add a habit" opens the add sheet; one small confetti burst on a perfect day. Looks, order, hidden ones and skips are synced in the `habit_board` setting, your own habits' look and schedule in `my_habits`. Smoke step "Today Now"; pictures: `TODAY_SHOTS=<dir> node e2e/todayShots.mjs`.
