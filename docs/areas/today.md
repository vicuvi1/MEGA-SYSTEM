### Pages and features


Today, Calendar, Tasks (Todoist), Goals, Focus, Progress, Reviews (month/year), Coach, Sleep (Waky embedded), **Discipline** (one built-in page; MAN is no longer embedded), Money (Finance Helper embedded), Body (Sport Track embedded), Rewards, **Notebook** (`pages/Notebook.tsx`, synced `notes` table; Ben can write notes too), Settings. Embedded apps live in `src/renderer/public/apps/` and talk to the host by `postMessage` (`LIFE_SYSTEM_BRIDGE`).

**Today → Now: Top 3, Other tasks, habits (a simple to-do list and tracker).** Top: one slim line (`TodayHero slim`): Ben's face, what's on now, "Next: <event> 13:30 in 3h", "Plan not approved [✓ Approve]" when Ben's plan waits, the reward you're saving for, and a ⋯ menu with Plan my day (`life-plan-day`), Shut down my day (`life-close-day`) and Life mode… (`life-mode-open`; `LifeModeCard quiet` shows nothing on a normal day). No score/focus/must-dos tiles and no separate NEXT strip on Now. Below, on a wide screen (the Now column at least 700 px) two cards side by side, stacked on a laptop or phone: **Top 3** (`habits/MustDos.tsx`) are the day plan's must-dos (the same list Ben, the island and Telegram use): big checks, type to add (up to 3), drag to reorder (`focus.setPlan` keeps done ones done), ★ takes one out (unstar in Todoist, or removes a typed one), a tick strikes the line and pops "✓ +10"; the first 3 pay +10 as before, a ⭐ Todoist task is completed in Todoist too; the week's big 3 is one small line in its header. **Other tasks** (`dayflow/OtherTasks.tsx`): the week's open big 3 ("This week", +25) then Todoist's due today and overdue in one list (overdue marked softly, time · length · area as small text), a circle completes it through the usual path (`life.todoist.complete`), ☆ promotes it to the Top 3, "Add a task…" adds it to Todoist for today, more than 8 fold into "Show N more"; full editing stays on the Tasks tab. **Habits** (`habits/HabitBoard.tsx`): one row per habit (icon, name, what's left or when it's due, last 7 days as dots, the streak, a circle to tick it), or a compact grid (⋯ → Show as a grid, per device `ls.hbView`); no Morning/Day/Evening boxes (one list: your order, else morning → evening, done ones last, `core/habitBoard.ts listTiles`). Auto habits (Workout from Sport Track, Focus from the timer, Log spending from Finance; a day with no spending ticks it with "No spending today" in the spending line or in Finance Helper, see rewards-money.md) show live progress ("0/50 min") and a tap only opens that app or timer: a tap never pays for something that didn't happen; the rules stay in `dayflow/hooks.ts` (`useHabits`) and the services. Long-press or a row's ⋯: skip today (no points, the streak waits), undo today (your own habits), details (28 days), edit, earlier / later (or drag), hide. The header: "Habits N of M", a thin bar, good days in a row, the money pill ("51 lei left today + Add") and ⋯ (add a habit, list/grid, keep the order fixed, hidden ones); "+ Add a habit" opens the add sheet; one small confetti burst on a perfect day. Looks, order, hidden ones and skips are synced in the `habit_board` setting, your own habits' look and schedule in `my_habits`. "Your day" (right column, `DayStrip.tsx`) shows each thing once (`core/dayColumn.ts dedupeColumn`): a task already in a block's checklist, or an old calendar event next to its moved task or copy, is one entry (same title, or the same words when the times overlap; ours first); two different things never merge. Smoke step "Today Now"; pictures: `TODAY_SHOTS=<dir> node e2e/todayShots.mjs`.

**Tasks sync with Todoist, both ways (2026-11-08; `core/todoSync.ts`, `web/todoistClient.ts`).** Every place that edits a task (the Tasks page, Today's Tasks tab, Top 3 ⭐, the plan card, Ben's quick commands and the island) goes through one object in the bridge, `life.todoist.update`. It sends only the fields that changed; "today" and "tomorrow" (also with a time) become your own calendar date on this device before they leave, and the server sends a plain date as Todoist's `due_date` (no reading in Todoist's account time zone). Priority has one mapping, `toApiPriority` / `fromApiPriority` in `core/todoist.ts`: P1 is Todoist's 4. Each edit waits in a queue until Todoist answers with the task (`/api/todoist {op:'update'}` returns `{ok, task}`); until then every list read from Todoist (`everything`, `today`, `upcoming`) shows your edit on top (a refresh that started before the write can't undo it), and a task you moved to today joins today's list at once. After the confirm, Todoist's answer is shown, and a later change made in Todoist wins. A failed save (offline, server busy, reads paused) stays in the queue, is kept on reload (`localStorage` `todoist_pending_edits`, this device), and is tried again with a growing wait, at once when the browser is back online, and before Sync; one Todoist refuses outright (task gone, bad value) is dropped with the error. On the Tasks page each row shows `saving…`, `✓ saved` (a few seconds) or `⚠ Couldn't save to Todoist. Retry`; the list reads are numbered so only the newest is shown, and a failed read keeps the list. Completing a task is unchanged (points only for a real tick). Smoke step "Tasks sync".

**Changes made in Todoist reach every surface (2026-11-09 audit).** The 5-second change check brings the changed tasks with it (`todoistClient.changes`), so within one check (at once with Sync) a task moved to another day, given or stripped of a time, left without a date, done, reopened, deleted, renamed, re-prioritised or moved to another project shows right on: the Tasks page and Today → Now's other tasks (they read again on `life-external-changed`), the Top 3 (linked must-dos follow, `core/todoFollow.ts` run by the bridge's `followTop3`: renamed → new name; moved away or deleted → off today's must-dos; done in Todoist → ticked, written with `FocusService.followTodoist`, which pays nothing: the Todoist task pays through its own rule), the day plan (planner.md, "plan sync rules"), the island list (it drops its 5-minute copy on `life-external-changed`) and Ben (his 45-second Todoist copy is dropped on `life-todoist-changed`). **Rule for an edit still waiting vs Todoist (`reconcilePatch`):** once Todoist's change check or a later read shows the task, a field Todoist already has is done, a field Todoist changed itself since your edit keeps Todoist's value (your part is dropped), a field Todoist still has at the old value is still sent, and a task done or deleted in Todoist drops the edit. An edit kept from an earlier visit is settled by the first read, so none waits forever. **Sync line:** "Synced 12:04 · 2 changes from Todoist" (only changes made in Todoist itself; the app's own edits coming back are not counted), and when Todoist does not answer the change check a Sync shows "Can't reach Todoist right now." with Retry; the auto line says "Can't reach Todoist, retrying" after 2 failed checks. Tests: `web/todoistE2e.test.ts` (a stateful fake Todoist driven through the real server code, `todoistOp`, the client queue and the change watch; a table of change → each surface). Smoke step "Todoist from outside".

**Sunday cards stay small (2026-11-08).** The smoke's clock fixes the time of day (08:30) but keeps the real date, so Sunday-only cards show up when the suite runs on a Sunday. On Now, "Pick your top 3 for this week" (`WeekTop3.tsx`, Sunday and Monday until picked) is one slim line under the top line (`WeekTop3Pick compact`, `.wk3-slim`): Pick opens the chips, Not now hides it for the week; the full card still shows in Ben's weekly push card. On Body, Ben's Sunday body report (`BodyWeekCard sunday folded`) is one folded line, "Next week: <focus>", with the whole report on tap; the Body page keeps the full card. Each Today tab still fits 1.75 laptop screens on any day. The habit step's "a second tap paid again" check compares the points number (`.tb-coins`) once the first payment's "+N" pop has gone, and fails if a new pop appears; it used to compare the whole top bar, pop included, which raced with the pop's 2.6 s timer. A second open of the Grooming checklist never calls `groomStep`, and `groomPay` pays only when the day is not paid yet.
