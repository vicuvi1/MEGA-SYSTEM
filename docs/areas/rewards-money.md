**Rewards (`pages/Rewards.tsx`, parts in `rewards/`).** A hero band (points, their lei value, today, the next reward at your pace, Ben's line), Surprises (level chests, the weekly mystery reward, coupons) and tabs For you · Unlock by doing · All · History · Earn. For you: Ready now (a row), Saving for with ¼ ½ ¾ milestones, and Suggested for you: a starter catalogue of about 50 rewards in four sizes (`core/rewardSuggest.ts`) ranked by what you logged this week (`core/rewardActivity.ts`: points by area, focus hours by kind from the subject, workouts, nights on time, water, saved music, fun time); one tap adds a reward with its healthy limits and lock. Unlock by doing: locks on a count of real logged things (`core/unlocks.ts` deed kind: focus or coding hours this week, workouts, nights on time, water days), checked again at the buy. Healthy limits (`core/rewardGuard.ts`, setting `reward_rules`): a most-per-day and "pair it" (only on a day with a workout, a move or a focus session), enforced in `Shop.redeem`; Ben's line warns when fun spending runs ahead of earning or the fun budget runs out early. Surprises stay honest (`core/rewardSurprise.ts`, setting `reward_coupons`): a chest gives its usual small coin bonus or a coupon (a free small treat from your own list, or 25% off); the mystery box (all of the week's top 3 done) gives a coupon only; undoing a buy gives its coupon back. Screenshots: `E2E_REWARDS_DEMO=1` on `e2e/server.mjs`.

**Game shop (2026-11).** The top of Rewards is now a shop front, always visible: the main saving goal as a bar with ¼ ½ ¾ marks, an "On a roll" combo (days in a row of real focus work), and "Spent this month N / M lei" (a soft meter from the existing `fun_budget` setting, default 500 lei, edited with Change; it warns and never blocks). The first tab is now **Shop** (id `now`): Pick of the day (a fixed pick by date among your rewards, same price, no discount), then shelves (`core/shopShelves.ts`: Big goals, Food, Games and screen, Movies and anime, Tech, Skincare and grooming, People, Treat yourself small). The shelf comes from the reward's category; four categories were added (`games`, `media`, `people`, `treat`), the old six map onto shelves (fun to Games, experience to Movies, gear to Tech, self-care to Skincare). Two shelves start open, the rest fold (remembered on the device). An empty shelf offers "Add starter items" (made-up names and prices you can edit; it only adds, never changes or removes saved rewards). Cards show a rarity by price (under 500 points common, 1,500 rare, 5,000 epic, then legendary), and a ring "340 / 600" when points are short. After a buy, a chest opens with a coin burst and the coin sound (skipped in calm mode and reduced motion). Nothing here makes points: buying still goes through `Shop.redeem` unchanged. Unlock by doing, All, History, Earn, Surprises and Suggested for you are all still there.
