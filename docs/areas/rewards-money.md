**Rewards (`pages/Rewards.tsx`, parts in `rewards/`).** A hero band (points, their lei value, today, the next reward at your pace, Ben's line; the small Next reward box names a different reward than the main goal and hides when there is none), one top row with Saving for (the main goal first, highlighted, with ¼ ½ ¾ milestones; the goal shows once at the top), On a roll and the fun budget, Surprises (level chests, the weekly mystery reward, coupons) and tabs For you · Unlock by doing · All · History · Earn. For you: Ready now (a row), and Suggested for you: a starter catalogue of about 50 rewards in four sizes (`core/rewardSuggest.ts`) ranked by what you logged this week (`core/rewardActivity.ts`: points by area, focus hours by kind from the subject, workouts, nights on time, water, saved music, fun time); one tap adds a reward with its healthy limits and lock. Unlock by doing: locks on a count of real logged things (`core/unlocks.ts` deed kind: focus or coding hours this week, workouts, nights on time, water days), checked again at the buy. Healthy limits (`core/rewardGuard.ts`, setting `reward_rules`): a most-per-day and "pair it" (only on a day with a workout, a move or a focus session), enforced in `Shop.redeem`; Ben's line warns when fun spending runs ahead of earning or the fun budget runs out early. Surprises stay honest (`core/rewardSurprise.ts`, setting `reward_coupons`): a chest gives its usual small coin bonus or a coupon (a free small treat from your own list, or 25% off); the mystery box (all of the week's top 3 done) gives a coupon only; undoing a buy gives its coupon back. Screenshots: `E2E_REWARDS_DEMO=1` on `e2e/server.mjs`.

The pick's shelf starts open; the other shelves fold into chips in one row (open state remembered on the device). Suggested for you is folded the same way. **One no-spend button: "Nothing spent today" (2026-11-12, replaces the free day).** There is one no-spend action, the honest one (`MoneyService.nothingSpent` in `src/main/money.ts`, `life.money.nothingSpent()`). It sits in the spending line under Habits on Today → Now (`data-testid="no-spend"`), on the Money page (Goals & points) and as a strip at the top of Finance Helper's Log (the `FINANCE_FREE_DAY` bridge message, op `mark` only; the answer `LIFE_FINANCE_FREE_DAYS` carries the marked days). It pays nothing at the tap and is refused once spending is logged that day; it saves the day in the synced `money_nothing_spent` setting. Once the day is over, `payDays` judges it on what was really logged and pays `money.no_spend` once (ref `fin:nospend:<day>`). The old "No spending today" free day (it paid "Spending logged" at once, with Undo) is removed: `freeDay` / `undoFreeDay` are gone from MoneyService, `webServices` and `life.finance`. Days it marked stay in `money_nothing_spent` and are judged like any other (paid once by ref); the points it already paid (ledger note `FREE_DAY_NOTE`) stay. Finance's Log list still shows such a day as "No spending ✓". Tests: `web/honestMoney.test.ts` ("one no-spend button"), smoke step "No spending".

**Buying with points is not spending (2026-11-08).** A reward bought with points no longer goes into Finance Helper. Before, `paid` defaulted to the reward's real cost, so every buy of a reward with a price guide (the starter "1 hour of gaming", 15 lei) landed in Finance as Fun/Reward spending, and Ben counted it in "today's spending". Now `Shop.redeem` and `life.shop.redeem` take `paid` = 0 unless you say otherwise. The buy sheet (`redeem.ts`, `askSwitch` in `confirmSheet.tsx`) shows "Get X for N points?" with an **I paid real money** switch, off at first; only when it is on is the typed amount recorded (`redemptions.real_cost`), checked against the fun budget and Safe to spend, and sent to Finance Helper as Fun under `life-reward-<ledgerId>` (undo takes it back out). The meter is renamed **Real money spent** (this month) and counts only those real-money buys (plus Finance Helper's Fun category when the budget comes from there). Ben's own "buy" tool is points only too. **Old entries:** Rewards shows "N reward buys are in Finance as real spending (X lei)" with each one tagged **From Rewards (points)**, a Remove per row and **Remove all reward entries from Finance**, each behind a confirm (`life.shop.rewardMoney()` / `removeRewardMoney(ids?)` in `webServices.ts`, rows from `rewardMoneyRows` in `core/funBudget.ts`: Finance records plus inbox entries not yet applied). Removing sends remove entries through the Finance inbox and sets those buys' `real_cost` to 0 (`Shop.clearPaid`); the buys stay in History and their points stay spent. Finance Helper's own list also tags `life-reward-…` records "From Rewards (points)" (`txRow` in `apps/finance/js/app.js`). Tests: `src/main/shop.test.ts`, `src/renderer/src/web/honestMoney.test.ts`; smoke "Rewards: shop shelves, budget meter and buy" (switch off by default, no new Finance entry after a buy).

**Money fixes (owner-approved, 2026-11-12).**
- **Foreign currency.** Rates are stored as units per 1 USD (`state.rates.usd`), so every conversion goes through `pairRate`. `fx.js rateToBase(state, cur)` is the one rule for a new record's rate: today's rate (4 decimals), else the rate of the latest record in that currency that was really converted, else null (never 1 just because no rate is known). Finance's `suggestedRate`, the Telegram / quick-add path (`web/financeBot.ts logMoney`, which used to look up `state.rates[cur]` and so counted 10 EUR as 10 MDL) and the previews use it. With no rate at all a bot record is kept at rate 1 and shows up in the re-price review.
- **Re-price past entries (one-time review).** `js/reprice.js` (pure) and `js/screens/reprice.js`. Home shows "N past entries in another currency were counted 1:1" with **Re-price N past entries** while any expense or income in another currency has `base === amount` and rate 1 or none. The sheet lists each with the amount, the old MDL value and the new one (that day's saved rate from `rates.history`, else today's); nothing changes until you confirm. Each changed record keeps `repricedFrom {rate, base, amount, currency, at}` and gets a new `updatedAt`, so the change (and an undo) syncs. **Undo** (toast, or "Review or undo" on Home) puts the old rate and value back exactly; a record edited after the re-price keeps the edit. Never applied on its own.
- **Imports never count 1:1.** `importer.js` keeps `rate: null` on rows in another currency without a rate; `rateGaps`, `fillRate`, `fillKnownRates`, `skipNoRate` and `screens/rategaps.js` give the CSV, the workbook and the bank statement previews a warning row per currency ("No EUR→MDL rate for 12 rows: they would count 1:1") with **Fetch rates**, **Set rate** (1 EUR = ? MDL) and **Skip these rows**; Import stays off until every such row has a rate or is skipped. The sheet-format CSV now takes the picked account's currency (as the workbook already did).
- **One spending rule.** `catshare.js countsAsSpending` (expenses, not loans, not `life-reward-…` points buys) is used by Finance's donut, its category list and the Money hub (`web/moneyHub.ts hubTx`), so "Where money goes" is the same total in both.
- **One money formatter.** `core/moneyText.ts` wraps Finance Helper's own `formatMoney`: `moneyText` for a single amount (keeps its bani: "12,50 MDL"), `totalText` / `totalNumber` for totals (whole units, like Finance's headline totals). The label is the currency code from Finance's `settings.currency` (MDL), never "lei". `core/moneyHub.ts leiText` now returns `totalText`, so Ben's money lines, the Money page, the Today pill and quick add all say "1 240 MDL". Quick add's preview and fly-in show the record's own currency (`currency` on `QuickPreview` / `QuickAdded`).
- **Faster saves.** Finance Helper writes through `store.js saveSoon` (one write for a burst of changes, `SAVE_DELAY` 400 ms) and `flushSave` on pagehide, beforeunload and when the tab is hidden; a direct `save`, a reset or encryption cancel or flush a waiting one, so an older copy never lands later. Life System hears `FINANCE_TRANSACTION_LOGGED` only after the write (it reads the stored copy to pay "Spending logged"). If the stored copy changes while a write waits (Life's sync, the Telegram inbox), the page merges it in first (`sync.js mergeLocal`: newest copy of each record wins, deletions stick). `appSync.ts` no longer turns the whole copy into its shared form on every save: it compares the raw text with the copy it last agreed on. The merge itself (read again inside the write, remembered deletions) is unchanged. Tests: `web/moneyFixes.test.ts` (two devices, a deletion, an offline edit, a merge while a save waits).
- **Telegram inbox warning.** `web/financeInbox.ts`: an entry older than 60 days is cleaned up only once Finance Helper has it (`keepInbox`, checked against the synced copy); one it does not have is never dropped. From day 50 the Money page shows "3 entries waiting from Telegram will expire in 10 days: apply now" (after day 60: "…are over 60 days old: apply now (kept until you do)") with **Apply now** (`life.finance.inboxWarning()` / `applyInbox()`); if they still cannot be added it says to open (and unlock) Finance Helper on this device.
- Smoke steps: "Money amounts" (10 EUR → 200 MDL, 12,50 MDL), "Finance import" (the warning, Set rate, Skip), "No spending".

**Money trust, phase 1: the money must always be right (owner-approved, 2026-11-12).**
- **Monthly reconciliation** (`apps/finance/js/reconcile.js`, pure; sheet `js/screens/reconcile.js`). Accounts → an account → **Reconcile** (or a chip in the "Reconcile: due" line at the top of Accounts): pick the day (default today), type the real balance from the bank or card, **Compare and save**. Each check is a reconciliation point in the synced list `state.reconciliations` (`{ id, account, date, real, app, diff, adjustment }`, in the account's currency; merged by id like records, removal leaves a `deleted` marker, in the undo history). With a difference the sheet lists the causes (`findCauses`): an entry of exactly that amount (or half of it: logged the wrong way), likely duplicates (same type and amount at most a day apart, same note or one without; parts of one split receipt are not paired), recurring items due and neither logged nor skipped (`recurring.js dueItems`), and every entry since the last point. Tapping an entry opens it to fix; comparing again shows what is left. Or **Add one adjustment of ±X**: one record, on the point's day, note "Reconciliation adjustment", `adjustment: true`, `reconcile: <point id>`, category Other / Other income, tagged "Reconciliation" in lists. It moves the balance only: it is neither income nor spending (`catshare.js isCorrection`, used by `countsAsSpending`, `countsAsIncome` and `model.js counts`). **Undo adjustment** (or the toast's Undo, or Ctrl+Z) removes it through `removeTx`; the point stays with its difference. A point can be removed (✕ in Past reconciliations; it takes its adjustment with it). Nothing here edits an existing record. **"Reconcile: due"**: an active account whose last point is more than 31 days old, or never reconciled with a first record more than 31 days old (`reconDue`); shown on Finance Accounts and on the Money page (Today tab, `hubView().reconcile`, `data-testid="reconcile-due"`). A tap there leaves `{ screen: 'reconcile', account }` under the local key `finance-helper.goto` and opens Finance Helper, which opens that account's reconcile sheet (read once, 10 minutes at most).
- **Money export** (`js/moneyexport.js`, pure; `js/screens/moneyio.js`). Settings → Data & backups → **Export all money (CSV + Excel)**: `money-transactions-<day>.csv` (UTF-8 with a BOM, CRLF, RFC 4180 quotes; a cell starting with = + - @ that is not a number gets a leading `'`) and `money-all-<day>.xlsx` (sheets Transactions, Accounts, Invoices, Clients, Recurring, Goals, Reconciliations). The transactions have every field: ID, date, type, account, from, to, to amount, category, subcategory, amount, currency, base amount (its MDL value), base currency, rate, note, tags (from the note), person, client, invoice id, loan, `repricedFrom` (JSON), recurring id, reconciliation id, created, updated, the raw account / category / person ids, and **Other fields** (JSON of anything else a record carries, e.g. `orig`, `debt`, `splitOf`, `source`), so the round trip loses nothing. Amounts are written `1250.50`.
- **Import of that CSV** (or the workbook's Transactions sheet): the file picker and paste in Log detect it by its header (`isMoneyExport`) and show a preview: new records, already here (same id, or the same date + type + amount + note + account as a record here), here with other values (kept as they are here: an import never overwrites), deleted on this device (never brought back), rows that could not be read (with the reason; a row whose account is not here is never put into another account). Import adds only the new rows, each with its id, rate and MDL value from the file and `updatedAt` = now (so it syncs); Undo takes them back out. The older "Export CSV" / sheet import is unchanged, except that a row in another currency now gets its own day's saved rate (`rateOnDay`), and the preview totals count each row at its rate (they added amounts of different currencies together).
- **Bugs fixed while proving the totals** (tests `web/moneyTrust.test.ts`, `web/moneyReconcile.test.ts`): (1) a reward bought with points (`life-reward-…`) still counted as spending in Finance's month summary, reports, the year review and the category list, while the Money hub left it out; (2) the same buys counted in safe to spend's "spent today" (`isEveryday`) and the forecast pace (`forecast.js`), so the hero disagreed with the hub's own list; (3) the Money hub's bills and month forecast took a recurring item in USD as MDL (10 USD = 10 MDL); (4) safe to spend counted money dated after today (logged ahead) as spendable now; (5) safe to spend and the rebuilt net worth (old months, the year review) priced foreign accounts with live rates only, so a dollar card counted as 0 there on a device without live rates while "All my money" counted it; (6) the import preview added amounts of different currencies together; (7) an old-format CSV import priced past rows at today's rate even when that day's rate was saved; (8) two devices that changed the same record in the same millisecond never agreed (each kept its own copy): the merge now picks the same copy on both.
- **Refunds:** there is no separate refund type yet. Money back is logged as income (Other income) in the account it came back to: the balance is exact and Finance and the hub agree (tested), but it shows as income rather than lowering that category's spending.
- Smoke steps: "Money reconcile" (Reconcile: due on the Money page, a 12,34 MDL difference, adjustment, Undo) and "Money export" (BOM, the columns, re-import: 0 new, every record already here).

**Money phase 4: the long view (read only, 2026-11-12).**
- **Where:** Finance → Reports → **Long view** (`#longview`, a third tab next to Year in review; `js/screens/longview.js`, styles `css/longview.css`, loaded by the screen). The numbers are `js/longview.js` (pure, `today` passed in, tested in `web/financeLongView.test.ts`). Nothing in this phase writes: no record, setting or new field changes.
- **Top row (4 numbers):** net worth and its change this year; this year's savings rate with the trend (last 3 full months against the 3 before, in points); spending so far against the same days of last year; runway.
- **Net worth over time** (`netWorthHistory`): one point per month end from the first record, plus year ends, as a chart and a table (Months / Years). A month with a saved snapshot (`state.netWorth`) and no records after it that month uses the snapshot (marked ●); other months are rebuilt from balances and records, foreign accounts at the saved rate of that day (`rates.history`, nearest earlier day), else the latest converted record in that currency, else `knownRates`; never 1:1 (an account with no rate is listed). Today's point is exactly `totalBalance` (open accounts, coins, `knownRates`); coins count only today. Tap a row: "What each account adds" lists each account's value and change for that month.
- **Savings rate** (`savingsRates`): (income − spending) ÷ income per month and per year, with `catshare.js countsAsIncome` / `countsAsSpending` (the one rule; transfers, loans, points buys and reconciliation adjustments are neither). A refund is logged as income, so it counts as income, the same as Reports. A month without income has no rate.
- **This year against last year** (`yearOverYear`): each spending category, **So far** (1 January to today against the same days last year; 29 February compares with 28 February) or **Full year**, with the 3 biggest increases and decreases; ‹ › picks the year.
- **Runway** (`runway`): liquid savings (open accounts except crypto, coins left out) ÷ average monthly spending over the last 3, 6 and 12 full months with records (a month with no records at all is a gap). In the first month: this month so far scaled to a month. The line is "You could live N months on your savings" (6 months; "7,5" below 10).
- **Currency:** MDL (the main currency) by default; **USD** and **EUR** (shown when a rate is known) convert every record and month end at the rate of its day and today at today's rate (`viewIn`, `viewCurrencies`).
- **Books:** reads `tx.book ?? 'personal'` and `account.book ?? 'personal'`; a book filter shows once more than one book exists. With a book picked, only its records count and an account's start counts in its own book, so the books add up to the whole; snapshots are not used in a book view.
- **Money page:** the Month tab has one **Long view** card (`MoneyLong.tsx`, `moneyLong.css`): net worth, this year's change, savings rate, runway and the runway line; **See it all** opens Finance on the Long view (`finance-helper.goto` `{ screen: 'longview' }`). The numbers come from `longCard` through `web/moneyLong.ts`, added in `web/moneyApi.ts` (not `moneyHub.ts`, so the bot bundle does not change).
