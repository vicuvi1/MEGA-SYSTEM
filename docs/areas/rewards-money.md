**Rewards (`pages/Rewards.tsx`, parts in `rewards/`).** A hero band (points, their lei value, today, the next reward at your pace, Ben's line; the small Next reward box names a different reward than the main goal and hides when there is none), one top row with Saving for (the main goal first, highlighted, with ¼ ½ ¾ milestones; the goal shows once at the top), On a roll and the fun budget, Surprises (level chests, the weekly mystery reward, coupons) and tabs For you · Unlock by doing · All · History · Earn. For you: Ready now (a row), and Suggested for you: a starter catalogue of about 50 rewards in four sizes (`core/rewardSuggest.ts`) ranked by what you logged this week (`core/rewardActivity.ts`: points by area, focus hours by kind from the subject, workouts, nights on time, water, saved music, fun time); one tap adds a reward with its healthy limits and lock. Unlock by doing: locks on a count of real logged things (`core/unlocks.ts` deed kind: focus or coding hours this week, workouts, nights on time, water days), checked again at the buy. Healthy limits (`core/rewardGuard.ts`, setting `reward_rules`): a most-per-day and "pair it" (only on a day with a workout, a move or a focus session), enforced in `Shop.redeem`; Ben's line warns when fun spending runs ahead of earning or the fun budget runs out early. Surprises stay honest (`core/rewardSurprise.ts`, setting `reward_coupons`): a chest gives its usual small coin bonus or a coupon (a free small treat from your own list, or 25% off); the mystery box (all of the week's top 3 done) gives a coupon only; undoing a buy gives its coupon back. Screenshots: `E2E_REWARDS_DEMO=1` on `e2e/server.mjs`.

**Game shop (2026-11).** The top of Rewards is now a shop front, always visible: the main saving goal as a bar with ¼ ½ ¾ marks, an "On a roll" combo (days in a row of real focus work), and "Real money spent N / M lei" (this month, only real money counts, see below; a soft meter from the existing `fun_budget` setting, default 500 lei, edited with Change; it warns and never blocks). The first tab is now **Shop** (id `now`): Today's pick (a fixed pick by date among your rewards, same price, no discount) is one highlighted card at the front of its own shelf, never shown twice; then shelves (`core/shopShelves.ts`: Big goals, Food, Games and screen, Movies and anime, Tech, Skincare and grooming, People, Treat yourself small). The shelf comes from the reward's category; four categories were added (`games`, `media`, `people`, `treat`), the old six map onto shelves (fun to Games, experience to Movies, gear to Tech, self-care to Skincare). The pick's shelf starts open; the other shelves fold into chips in one row (open state remembered on the device). Suggested for you is folded the same way. An empty shelf offers "Add starter items" (made-up names and prices you can edit; it only adds, never changes or removes saved rewards). Every card shows lei as points / 10 (the owner's rule, `POINTS_PER_LEI`); starter items are priced real lei x 10 (pizza night 150 lei = 1,500 points). Cards show a rarity by price (under 500 points common, 1,500 rare, 5,000 epic, then legendary), and a ring "340 / 600" when points are short. After a buy, a chest opens with a coin burst and the coin sound (skipped in calm mode and reduced motion). Nothing here makes points: buying still goes through `Shop.redeem` unchanged. Unlock by doing, All, History, Earn, Surprises and Suggested for you are all still there.

**One no-spend button: "Nothing spent today" (2026-11-12, replaces the free day).** There is one no-spend action, the honest one (`MoneyService.nothingSpent` in `src/main/money.ts`, `life.money.nothingSpent()`). It sits in the spending line under Habits on Today → Now (`data-testid="no-spend"`), on the Money page (Goals & points) and as a strip at the top of Finance Helper's Log (the `FINANCE_FREE_DAY` bridge message, op `mark` only; the answer `LIFE_FINANCE_FREE_DAYS` carries the marked days). It pays nothing at the tap and is refused once spending is logged that day; it saves the day in the synced `money_nothing_spent` setting. Once the day is over, `payDays` judges it on what was really logged and pays `money.no_spend` once (ref `fin:nospend:<day>`). The old "No spending today" free day (it paid "Spending logged" at once, with Undo) is removed: `freeDay` / `undoFreeDay` are gone from MoneyService, `webServices` and `life.finance`. Days it marked stay in `money_nothing_spent` and are judged like any other (paid once by ref); the points it already paid (ledger note `FREE_DAY_NOTE`) stay. Finance's Log list still shows such a day as "No spending ✓". Tests: `web/honestMoney.test.ts` ("one no-spend button"), smoke step "No spending".

**Buying with points is not spending (2026-11-08).** A reward bought with points no longer goes into Finance Helper. Before, `paid` defaulted to the reward's real cost, so every buy of a reward with a price guide (the starter "1 hour of gaming", 15 lei) landed in Finance as Fun/Reward spending, and Ben counted it in "today's spending". Now `Shop.redeem` and `life.shop.redeem` take `paid` = 0 unless you say otherwise. The buy sheet (`redeem.ts`, `askSwitch` in `confirmSheet.tsx`) shows "Get X for N points?" with an **I paid real money** switch, off at first; only when it is on is the typed amount recorded (`redemptions.real_cost`), checked against the fun budget and Safe to spend, and sent to Finance Helper as Fun under `life-reward-<ledgerId>` (undo takes it back out). The meter is renamed **Real money spent** (this month) and counts only those real-money buys (plus Finance Helper's Fun category when the budget comes from there). Ben's own "buy" tool is points only too. **Old entries:** Rewards shows "N reward buys are in Finance as real spending (X lei)" with each one tagged **From Rewards (points)**, a Remove per row and **Remove all reward entries from Finance**, each behind a confirm (`life.shop.rewardMoney()` / `removeRewardMoney(ids?)` in `webServices.ts`, rows from `rewardMoneyRows` in `core/funBudget.ts`: Finance records plus inbox entries not yet applied). Removing sends remove entries through the Finance inbox and sets those buys' `real_cost` to 0 (`Shop.clearPaid`); the buys stay in History and their points stay spent. Finance Helper's own list also tags `life-reward-…` records "From Rewards (points)" (`txRow` in `apps/finance/js/app.js`). Tests: `src/main/shop.test.ts`, `src/renderer/src/web/honestMoney.test.ts`; smoke "Rewards: shop shelves, budget meter and buy" (switch off by default, no new Finance entry after a buy).

**Finance: "Where your money goes" donut (2026-11-09).** Finance Home (right after the month summary) and Reports show a donut of spending by category with "Spent N MDL" in the middle, and under it one rounded row per category (icon, name, amount, share in a small circle, a thin bar, and "+12% vs last month" in grey). Biggest first. Periods: This month, Last month, 3 months, Year, plus month arrows (`ui.spendPeriod`, `m:YYYY-MM` for a picked month). The comparison is with the period before, and for a period still running only the same number of days (so "this month" is not judged against a whole month). Left out: transfers, income, loans and the `life-reward-…` records ("From Rewards (points)"). Categories under 3% (when there are two or more), and anything past 7 segments, fold into **Other**; tapping it opens the folded ones. Tapping a segment, label or row lights it up and lists its records for the period; **See all in the Log** opens the Log with `cat:<id> from:… to:…`. The numbers are `js/catshare.js` (pure, tested in `web/financeCategoryShares.test.ts`); the card and the inline-SVG donut (stroked circles, draw-in animation that respects reduced motion, a hidden table for screen readers, leader-line labels only when the card is wide) are in `js/screens/spending.js`, styles `.dn-*` in `css/app.css`. Colours are the `--c1..--c8` category tokens, the same per category as in the Log. Smoke step "Finance categories".

**Money fixes (owner-approved, 2026-11-12).**
- **Foreign currency.** Rates are stored as units per 1 USD (`state.rates.usd`), so every conversion goes through `pairRate`. `fx.js rateToBase(state, cur)` is the one rule for a new record's rate: today's rate (4 decimals), else the rate of the latest record in that currency that was really converted, else null (never 1 just because no rate is known). Finance's `suggestedRate`, the Telegram / quick-add path (`web/financeBot.ts logMoney`, which used to look up `state.rates[cur]` and so counted 10 EUR as 10 MDL) and the previews use it. With no rate at all a bot record is kept at rate 1 and shows up in the re-price review.
- **Re-price past entries (one-time review).** `js/reprice.js` (pure) and `js/screens/reprice.js`. Home shows "N past entries in another currency were counted 1:1" with **Re-price N past entries** while any expense or income in another currency has `base === amount` and rate 1 or none. The sheet lists each with the amount, the old MDL value and the new one (that day's saved rate from `rates.history`, else today's); nothing changes until you confirm. Each changed record keeps `repricedFrom {rate, base, amount, currency, at}` and gets a new `updatedAt`, so the change (and an undo) syncs. **Undo** (toast, or "Review or undo" on Home) puts the old rate and value back exactly; a record edited after the re-price keeps the edit. Never applied on its own.
- **Imports never count 1:1.** `importer.js` keeps `rate: null` on rows in another currency without a rate; `rateGaps`, `fillRate`, `fillKnownRates`, `skipNoRate` and `screens/rategaps.js` give the CSV, the workbook and the bank statement previews a warning row per currency ("No EUR→MDL rate for 12 rows: they would count 1:1") with **Fetch rates**, **Set rate** (1 EUR = ? MDL) and **Skip these rows**; Import stays off until every such row has a rate or is skipped. The sheet-format CSV now takes the picked account's currency (as the workbook already did).
- **One spending rule.** `catshare.js countsAsSpending` (expenses, not loans, not `life-reward-…` points buys) is used by Finance's donut, its category list and the Money hub (`web/moneyHub.ts hubTx`), so "Where money goes" is the same total in both.
- **One money formatter.** `core/moneyText.ts` wraps Finance Helper's own `formatMoney`: `moneyText` for a single amount (keeps its bani: "12,50 MDL"), `totalText` / `totalNumber` for totals (whole units, like Finance's headline totals). The label is the currency code from Finance's `settings.currency` (MDL), never "lei". `core/moneyHub.ts leiText` now returns `totalText`, so Ben's money lines, the Money page, the Today pill and quick add all say "1 240 MDL". Quick add's preview and fly-in show the record's own currency (`currency` on `QuickPreview` / `QuickAdded`).
- **Faster saves.** Finance Helper writes through `store.js saveSoon` (one write for a burst of changes, `SAVE_DELAY` 400 ms) and `flushSave` on pagehide, beforeunload and when the tab is hidden; a direct `save`, a reset or encryption cancel or flush a waiting one, so an older copy never lands later. Life System hears `FINANCE_TRANSACTION_LOGGED` only after the write (it reads the stored copy to pay "Spending logged"). If the stored copy changes while a write waits (Life's sync, the Telegram inbox), the page merges it in first (`sync.js mergeLocal`: newest copy of each record wins, deletions stick). `appSync.ts` no longer turns the whole copy into its shared form on every save: it compares the raw text with the copy it last agreed on. The merge itself (read again inside the write, remembered deletions) is unchanged. Tests: `web/moneyFixes.test.ts` (two devices, a deletion, an offline edit, a merge while a save waits).
- **Telegram inbox warning.** `web/financeInbox.ts`: an entry older than 60 days is cleaned up only once Finance Helper has it (`keepInbox`, checked against the synced copy); one it does not have is never dropped. From day 50 the Money page shows "3 entries waiting from Telegram will expire in 10 days: apply now" (after day 60: "…are over 60 days old: apply now (kept until you do)") with **Apply now** (`life.finance.inboxWarning()` / `applyInbox()`); if they still cannot be added it says to open (and unlock) Finance Helper on this device.
